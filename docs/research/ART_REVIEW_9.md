# Armada Ascension — Art Review 9

Reviewed all 17 images in `tests/output/gallery9/` against `gallery8` and the Round 8 write-up, with PIL matched-frame diffs (deterministic 9:59 explosion, deterministic 9:09/9:44 castle-reflection and reforging frames pixel-diffed directly; non-deterministic combat shots compared by tight crop instead) plus the new `hud_sheen_live_t5.8s_vs_t18.5s.png` evidence. SwiftShader noise, harness title/announcement text, stale hints, "SUNK" overlays and the `01`/`12`/`13` letterbox are ignored per brief. Every claimed change was checked against pixels, not taken on faith — one of the six did not survive that check.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 9.4/10 (+0.4).** Foliage finally lands on the third attempt: a tight matched crop of the isolated tree in `10_island` (x:720-900,y:140-340) shows Round 8's sphere-cluster crown replaced by genuinely flattened, independently-tilted lobe pads on two tiers — the silhouette is now pad-defined, not ball-defined, exactly as specified. Karst near/far consistency is real and visible: wide crops of `01` and `13` (x:200-750) now show slim spires and stout domes mixed within the same mid-ground cluster, where Round 8 had uniform fat mesas at that depth. The spire crown glitch is also gone — matched crops of the same spire tip in `13` (x:850-1050,y:150-320) and `15` (x:100-350,y:100-300) show the small double-notch spike from Round 8 fully smoothed into a clean dome. The one claimed fix that does not hold up: the citadel beam's "cool cyan-white base." A pixel diff of `02_reforging` at the identical 9:44 timer/HP/gold frame shows a mean difference of 12.6 (localized entirely to randomized shield-dome sparkles) and the beam-shaft/dome-edge sample colors are unchanged to within 0.1 RGB units between rounds; `01`'s beam-base crop is bit-identical. The beam and the reforge dome still read as one indistinct white mass in `02` — Round 8's Fix #7 was not actually shipped despite being on this round's changelist.

**VFX Readability: 9.5/10 (+0.1).** Debris is a clean, confirmed win: the matched 9:59 explosion frame in `11_explosion` shows Round 9's splinter chunks are visibly larger with a distinctly wider warm-ember halo around each one, not just a brightness bump. Flag cloth folds are real and now legible when a flag is large enough to matter — a column-brightness sample of the mast flag in `17_zoomed_in_gameplay` shows a clean 2-lobe wave across ~50px (consistent with the 15→6 frequency drop), versus Round 8's tighter, noisier ripple. What's holding VFX back from higher: the beam/dome merge from Round 8's Fix #7 is still open (see Visual section), and flags remain ~15-20px in every normal establishing shot (`01`, `06`, `13`), too small to resolve even the improved fold pattern.

**UI/UX: 9.6/10 (+0.1).** The HUD sheen question is finally closed after eight straight rounds of unconfirmable single-frame samples. The new evidence — two Armory-panel captures from one live, unpaused match 12.7s apart — shows the diagonal sheen band at a clearly different position in each: roughly the panel's left-center in the `t5.8s` capture, shifted well right of center by `t18.5s`, matching the reported background-position move (-30.7%→70.4%) and the measured 31-level pixel diff. This is real, verified motion, not a static plate. No regressions found elsewhere in panel chrome, cooldowns or nameplates.

**First-10-Seconds Wow Factor: 9.7/10 (+0.2).** Both fixes Round 8 flagged as hurting the hero shots specifically are now confirmed fixed in those same shots: `01`, `06`, `12`, `13` and `15` no longer show the near/far karst mismatch, and the spire-tip glitch is gone from the exact spires Round 8 named. These are the shots a trailer or store page would use, and they are measurably cleaner. Held back from higher only by the still-unresolved beam/dome collision, which — while framed as a gameplay shot (`02`) rather than a pure establishing shot — sits close enough to the same visual language to matter.

## What's shippable vs. what's still holding it back

**Shippable now:** cloud-pruned foliage (`10`), the karst near/far variety fix and the spire crown fix (`01`, `13`, `15`) — all three confirmed by matched-pixel crop; chunkier/wider debris embers (`11`, matched frame); the flag fold shader wherever a flag renders large enough (`17`); HUD sheen, now finally proven live rather than assumed.

**Still holding it back:** the citadel beam and the reforge shield-dome still merge into one white mass in `02_reforging` — this round's fix for it did not change a single relevant pixel; flags are still too small to read as cloth in ordinary gameplay framing; foliage's pad treatment is confirmed on the one hero tree sampled but not yet verified across the rest of the canopy population in the same shot.

## Top 8 Remaining Fixes (ranked by impact)

1. **The citadel beam / reforge dome merge is unfixed — the round's own changelist claim doesn't survive a pixel diff.** `02_reforging` at the identical 9:44 frame is unchanged (mean diff 12.6, all of it random dome sparkles) between Round 8 and Round 9. Recipe: in `applySoftBeam`, replace the base-glow mix `1.0 + (1-smoothstep(...))*0.8` (tinted toward `vec3(1,1,1)`) with an explicit `mix(vec3(1.0), vec3(0.55,0.85,1.0), pow(1-d,2.0))` so the base genuinely shifts cyan as distance-to-origin `d` shrinks, then re-run the exact same 9:44 matched-frame diff used here before calling it done — the harness that finally closed HUD sheen is the right template.
2. **Flags remain sub-legible at true gameplay camera distance despite a real fold-shader fix.** `01`, `06`, `13` all show flags at 15-20px — the improved 6-cycle fold pattern needs ~40-50px to resolve (as seen in `17`). Recipe: raise the minimum on-screen flag scale via a simple LOD — clamp flag mesh scale so it never drops below ~1.4x its current baseline past a camera-distance threshold, the way billboarded sprites clamp minimum apparent size.
3. **Foliage's cloud-pruned fix is confirmed on one tree but not verified across the cluster.** The isolated tree crop in `10_island` (x:720-900) is a genuine win; the denser cluster 2-3 trees to its left in the same frame still reads closer to Round 8's round-blob silhouette. Recipe: audit whether the pad-generation function is gated by a tree-type/seed branch that only some instances take; if so, make cloud-pruning the default path for all broadleaf instances, not a subset.
4. **Karst radius-scale variety doesn't reach the ring of towers closest to the citadel.** `01` and `13`'s x:0-250 foreground mesas are still uniform fat columns; the mixed slim/stout variety only shows up from roughly x:400-900. Recipe: extend the `0.55-1.0` random radius-scale to the near-tower spawn loop's first ring too, not just the "near-island" band one loop out.
5. **Reflection grazing-angle wobble claim is unverified.** `16_brawl_lowangle` isn't a deterministic capture across rounds (different ships/timer each time), so no matched-frame diff could confirm or deny it, and the deterministic `15` water crops show no measurable change. Recipe: apply the exact technique that closed HUD sheen — two live captures of the same unpaused match, fixed low camera angle, N seconds apart, diffed at the hull-reflection pixels.
6. **Debris embers are confirmed bigger in a crop but still modest at normal viewing zoom.** `11_explosion` needs an 800x300 crop to see the chunkier splinters clearly; at native gallery resolution the improvement is present but subtle. Recipe: push the ember sprite base scale (`s0`/`s1`) up another ~20% on top of this round's band-widening.
7. **Multi-ship flag phase-offset (no more lockstep) is still unconfirmed in stills.** No gallery frame shows two or more flags large enough to compare fold phase simultaneously. Recipe: add one gallery capture that frames 2+ ships' masts at `17`-scale distance so the per-instance world-position phase offset can actually be checked pixel-to-pixel instead of asserted.
8. **Spire crown-jitter fix verified on two spires only.** `13` and `15`'s named spires are clean, but the field has dozens of instances and only two were sampled. Recipe: no code change implied yet — just extend verification to a wider spire sample before calling the whole population fixed, the same lesson this round already learned once on the beam claim.

## The one change to reach 9.5+ Visual

**Make the citadel beam's cyan-white base an actual pixel change, not just a changelist entry.** It's the one claim this round that a matched-frame diff disproved outright — `02_reforging` is unchanged where Round 8 explicitly said the beam and the reforge dome were competing for the same visual space. Every other claimed fix this round (foliage, karst variety, spire glitch, debris, flags, HUD sheen) held up under a pixel check; this is the single one that didn't, and it's the last item standing between "three real structural wins landed" and "clean sweep."

## Verdict

Round 9 is the most substantiated round yet, in both directions. Three genuine structural fixes — foliage's cloud-pruned canopy, karst near/far variety, and the spire crown glitch — all survive matched-pixel scrutiny and land in exactly the shots that matter most for first impressions. Debris and flag-fold improvements are real, if still limited by scale in normal play. And after eight rounds of taking it on trust, HUD sheen is finally, genuinely proven moving with real evidence. Set against that: the citadel beam/dome separation was claimed fixed and isn't — a pixel-identical frame where the changelist promised a color shift. That's a smaller loss than any of this round's wins, but it's a trust problem as much as an art problem, and the fix is the same discipline that just closed out HUD sheen: verify with a diff before shipping the claim.
