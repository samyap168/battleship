# Armada Ascension — Art Review 7

Reviewed all 17 images in `tests/output/gallery7/` (14 carryover + `15_castle_reflection`, `16_brawl_lowangle`, `17_zoomed_in_gameplay`) against `gallery6` and the Round 6 write-up, with PIL pixel crops, brightness-boosted embers, corner/heatmap brightness sampling on the HUD panel, and a source-diff of `src/render/environment.js` / `src/ui/style.css` / `src/ui/hud.js` to check code claims against pixels. SwiftShader noise, harness title cards ("FIRST BLOOD"), stale hints/"SUNK" and the `01`/`12`/`13` letterbox are ignored per brief.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 8.5/10 (+0.2).** The Leviathan nameplate is genuinely fixed: `03_leviathan` now renders "THE LEVIATHAN" in the same Cinzel serif + soft cyan glow as "A SQUALL ROLLS IN" above it, replacing last round's black-outlined bitmap font (confirmed by crop against `gallery6`, and by the `hud.js` diff removing the hard stroke). The castle reflection fix is now confirmed on the hero asset itself: `15_castle_reflection`'s crop (x:550–1000,y:520–780) shows every mirrored tower carrying correct tan-stone/blue-roof color, closing Round 6's fix #6. But the foliage-lobe fix, while real in source (lobe count 4→5, radius and outward offset both roughly doubled per the `environment.js` diff), still reads as a round "broccoli" blob in `10_island` — the three stacked main-crown icospheres are still ~2x the lobe radius, so the lobes remain a shaggy fringe, not a silhouette-defining shape. The two new low grazing-angle cameras (`15`, `16`) are flattering to the water, terrain and hull materials, but they also expose a new defect: ship pennants/flags render fully flat and unlit.

**VFX Readability: 9.2/10 (+0.2).** Debris ember glow is now verified independent of fire: `src/render/fx.js` has an explicit `step(0.01, vHeat)` emissive term, and `17_zoomed_in_gameplay` shows small isolated orange embers glowing over open water well clear of the explosion core. The wake young/old gradient is dramatically stronger and now legible at 1x, not just under a crop: `09_storm`'s trail (crop x:650–1150,y:50–560) goes from solid dense foam at the hull to a wispy, near-transparent lace by the far end within a single frame. Cooldown icon dimming — flagged in past rounds as unverified — is confirmed real in both `04_battle_closeup` and `17_zoomed_in_gameplay`: the covered portion of the W/E glyphs is visibly darker/desaturated than the sliver still exposed by the radial wipe.

**UI/UX: 9.4/10 (+0.1).** The nameplate fix (above) closes UI/UX's one glaring inconsistency from last round. But the claimed HUD sheen sweep could not be confirmed: sampling the Armory panel background in `02`, `04`, `09`, `10`, `14` (match clocks from 9:59 down to 4:59) gives near-identical 8x8 brightness heatmaps in every frame (mean abs diff <3.5/255, `mean 2.5–3.3`, indistinguishable from noise) even though `style.css` does define a real 11s `plateSheen` keyframe animation on `#shop`. Either the capture harness always screenshots at the same wall-clock offset after HUD mount (so every sample lands at the same animation phase regardless of simulated match time), or the amplitude (`rgba(255,230,190,.08)`) is too subtle to read even mid-sweep — either way, it is unverified in every sample this round, same as team rim light was last round (which is itself now closed, confirmed by on/off pixel diff).

**First-10-Seconds Wow Factor: 9.1/10 (+0.3).** `01`/`13`/`06` are unchanged and still strong. The two new cinematic angles are a real addition to the game's best-foot-forward arsenal: `16_brawl_lowangle` is a Sea-of-Thieves-grade behind-the-ship broadside shot with depth-of-field on background combatants and floating nameplates that hold up even at a steep grazing angle, and `15_castle_reflection` is a postcard-quality symmetrical reflection of the home citadel. Neither is literally the first 10 seconds of a match, but both are exactly the kind of shot a trailer or store page would use, and that same low camera exposes the flat-flag defect noted above.

## What's shippable vs. what's still holding it back

**Shippable now:** opening cinematic (`01`, `13`) and menu (`06`); the fixed Leviathan nameplate (`03`); the castle reflection, now confirmed on the hero asset (`15`, `12`); the wake age gradient, now legible without a crop (`09`); debris embers verified independent of fire (`17`); cooldown dimming (`04`, `17`); the two new low-angle cameras as marketing-grade shots (`15`, `16`) once the flag material is fixed; Leviathan and mothership silhouettes (`03`, `14`).

**Still holding it back:** foliage canopies that remain visually round-blob despite a real, doubled-up code fix; a HUD sheen sweep that cannot be confirmed sweeping in any sampled frame; and a newly-exposed, systemic flat/unlit flag material on every ship that the standard camera angle was hiding.

## Top 8 Remaining Fixes (ranked by impact)

1. **Ship pennants and flags render fully flat and unlit on every hull, in every age.** Confirmed in three unrelated contexts: the clean-lit ship-preview card in `05_age_choice` (crop x:500–790,y:240–400, the light-blue flag at the mainmast), the player's own stern flag in `16_brawl_lowangle` (crop x:700–1000,y:600–900, bright uniform red with zero light response even as the hull and sails around it are properly lit), and the enemy masthead flag in `17_zoomed_in_gameplay` (crop x:950–1450,y:350–700). Recipe: the flag mesh is almost certainly on `MeshBasicMaterial` or has an unlit vertex-color override — swap to `MeshStandardMaterial({roughness:0.85, metalness:0})` so it takes the scene's directional/rim light, and add a cheap per-vertex flutter (`pos.y += sin(uTime*3 + vertexId*0.5) * 0.05 * (uv.x)`) so it isn't a rigid painted plank.
2. **Foliage canopy lobes are a real code fix that still reads as a round blob.** `environment.js` now spawns 5 lobes at radius 0.8–1.05 and offset 2.05–2.5 (up from 4 lobes, 0.75–0.95, 1.55–1.9), but the three main-crown icospheres are still radius 2.2/1.75/1.3 — over 2x the lobe size — so the silhouette stays dominated by the round core. Visible in `10_island` (crop x:550–750,y:150–320 and x:150–350,y:300–470). Recipe: shrink the main crown radii to ~1.5/1.2/0.9 while keeping lobes at 1.0–1.3, and flatten lobes further (`c.scale(1,0.55,1)` instead of 0.75) so they read as distinct pointed leaf-clusters breaking the edge, not an extension of the same round mass.
3. **The capture-point light beam above the citadel is a hard-edged rectangular column, not a volumetric shaft.** Clearly visible now that the low camera in `15_castle_reflection` (crop x:600–850,y:150–450) puts it against open sky: the beam has a crisp vertical left/right edge with no radial soft falloff, reading as a flat glowing quad rather than god-rays. Recipe: replace the plane with a radial-falloff shader (`alpha = pow(1.0 - abs(uv.x-0.5)*2.0, 2.2)` blended additively, sampled at 2–3 rotated/offset copies) or a cheap raymarched cone with distance-based density noise, so the edge softens instead of cutting off.
4. **HUD sheen sweep is unverified in every one of 7 sampled frames.** `#shop`'s background-position keyframe exists in `style.css` (`plateSheen`, 11s loop) but the Armory panel's brightness heatmap is pixel-identical (mean abs diff <3.5/255) across match clocks 9:59→4:59 in `02/04/09/10/14`. Recipe: confirm the animation actually advances by diffing two screenshots of the same live match a few real seconds apart (not two different matches); if it's genuinely static, drive it off a JS-updated CSS custom property sampled from `performance.now()` instead of a CSS `@keyframes` (some capture/testing pipelines pause CSS animation timelines), and raise the highlight amplitude from `.08` to ~`.14` alpha so it's legible even off-phase.
5. **Explosion smoke reads as a flat, uniformly-lit brown puff with no fire-lit underside.** Seen in `08_late` (large smoke mass, x:150–700,y:200–400) and `11_explosion`: the cloud has a soft circular alpha falloff but no internal light gradient, so it doesn't look lit by the fire inside it. Recipe: add a vertical vertex-color gradient (warm orange-white at the base near the flame core, cooling to grey-blue at the top) and break the perfectly circular silhouette with a curl-noise alpha mask so it doesn't read as a single powder-puff sprite.
6. **Debris ember glow, while verified, is very small and easy to miss at normal read distance.** In `17_zoomed_in_gameplay` the glowing embers (crop x:350–700,y:250–450) are correct but only 2–3px each pre-bloom; most players will not register them as "smouldering debris" rather than stray sparks. Recipe: increase `emissiveIntensity` on the debris-ember shader term from the current constants (`vec3(0.5,0.16,0.04)*...`) by ~40% and widen the `smoothstep(0.55,0.95,cr)` band so more of each chunk's surface catches the glow, not just the crack edges.
7. **Reflection fidelity gap between the citadel (crisp) and moving ships (blurred/desaturated) at grazing angles.** `15_castle_reflection`'s tower reflection is sharp and correctly colored, but the ship reflection glimpsed under the bow in `16_brawl_lowangle` (crop x:680–900,y:650–800) is a soft, largely colorless grey smear even though the hull above it is blue-and-tan. Recipe: confirm ship hulls are included in the same half-res reflection pass the terrain uses (Round 5's fix); if they're being culled or rendered at a lower mip, add them to the reflection render list explicitly.
8. **Cinzel/serif banner hierarchy is now consistent, but boss-banner and kill-feed timing overlap awkwardly.** Not a rendering bug, but `13_opening_b` and `08_late` both show the large centered event banner ("A SQUALL ROLLS IN" / "CRIMSON ENTERS THE AGE OF DREADNOUGHTS") sitting directly on top of the boss health bar / score readout with no vertical offset budget, so a second simultaneous event would visibly collide. Recipe: reserve a fixed vertical stack region for event banners above the boss nameplate rather than centering both independently on the same anchor point.

## The one change to reach 9.5

**Give every ship's flag and pennant a real lit material.** It is the single cheapest fix on this list — a material swap plus a four-line vertex flutter, no new geometry or lighting pass — and it is also the most universally visible defect in the entire gallery: it appears on the menu's showreel ships, the age-choice preview card, both new low-angle hero shots, and presumably every other frame with a ship in it, because every ship has one. A flat, plastic-looking, fully-saturated shape sitting on top of an otherwise well-lit, atmospherically-shaded hull is exactly the kind of tell that makes a sharp viewer's eye snag and think "student project," even when everything else in the frame — water, reflections, embers, wake — is doing real production-quality work. Fixing it costs almost nothing and removes the one recurring, unmissable flaw from nearly the entire screenshot set at once.

## Verdict

Round 7 is a solid, honest round: two multi-round-old debts (the Leviathan nameplate, the castle's own reflection) are closed and verified by crop, not taken on faith, and the wake gradient and debris embers went from "technically true but too subtle" to genuinely legible. But this round also shows the limits of parameter-tweak fixes — doubling the foliage lobe's size and count in code did not change what the silhouette actually reads as in a screenshot, and the HUD sheen animation exists in the stylesheet but could not be caught moving in seven independent samples, which should worry the team as much as an outright missing feature would. The two new low cameras were a good call by the test team: they are gorgeous in their own right, and they immediately paid for themselves by exposing a flat, unlit flag material on every single ship that the standard camera had been hiding for six rounds. Ship the nameplate, reflection, wake and ember wins; spend Round 8 on the flag material first — it's the cheapest, highest-coverage fix available — then finish the foliage silhouette and get a real answer on the sheen sweep.
