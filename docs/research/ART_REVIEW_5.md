# Armada Ascension — Art Review 5

Reviewed every image in `tests/output/gallery5/` against `gallery4` and the Round 4 write-up, with pixel-level crops (`PIL` zoom, 2–4x) on HUD panels, hulls, foliage, wakes, explosions and reflections rather than eyeballing full frames alone. SwiftShader noise, harness title cards, stale hints and "SUNK" overlays ignored per brief. The Round 4 "72% black frame" is confirmed gone: `01_opening_cinematic` and `13_opening_b` both render full-frame, correctly, with only the intentional letterbox bars as pure black.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 7.8/10 (+0.3).** Hull weathering is now a real, legible value-read at normal battle-cam zoom — the rust/grime patches on the ship in `04_battle_closeup` and the red cruiser in `08_late` read clearly without director-zoom, closing Round 4 fix #6. Island cliffs in `01`/`12`/`13` now show genuine horizontal sedimentary strata banding (confirmed by crop), and tree bark reads warm brown instead of the previous flat red-brown. But two claimed changes don't survive a pixel comparison: cropping the same rock spire and its waterline in `12_reflections` against `gallery4/12_reflections` produces near-identical images — no mirrored silhouette gain — and the foliage crop in `10_island` is essentially pixel-for-pixel the same blobby "cauliflower" canopy as `gallery4/10_island`, so "higher-contrast leaf clumps" is not a visible win.

**VFX Readability: 8.9/10 (+0.4).** The cooldown UI now genuinely shows a dark occlusion disc with the numeral timer sitting on it (`04_battle_closeup` Q/W/E crop), a real upgrade from Round 4's bare radial wipe. Whitecaps in `07_mid` are confirmed sparse and clustered — sunlit foam patches scattered across open water instead of a uniform crest line. The explosion in `11_explosion` shows a genuinely torn, lacy foam ring with gaps and irregular edges versus `gallery4`'s smoother donut, and its main fireball now throws visible ember-glowing splinter debris. Wake foam behind ships (`04`, `09`) reads as a jagged, veined broken trail rather than a net, though it's thinner/wispier than "clumps" implies.

**UI/UX: 9.0/10 (+/-0.0).** Genuine addition: small brass rivet dots now run along the Armory panel border and corner brackets (`hud_armory` crop vs `gallery4`), a real if modest step up from the bare gold hairline. But the claimed "bevel and sheen" material never arrives — the panel body itself is still the same flat vertical-gradient fill as Round 4, just with rivets glued to the frame, so the two-rounds-old "no baked material" defect (Round 3 fix #7, Round 4 fix #3) is still not actually fixed, just decorated. Kill-feed and objective banners (`03`, `08`) remain clean and well-hierarchized.

**First-10-Seconds Wow Factor: 8.0/10 (+2.5).** This is the headline win: `01_opening_cinematic` and `13_opening_b` now render the full establishing shot every time — warm sunrise shaft, archipelago silhouette, castle, and fleet sailing into frame, matching the quality Round 4 could only get from `12_reflections`. That single fix resolves the worst defect in three rounds of review. It doesn't reach a 9 because `06_menu` — the only other "first impression" frame this round — is a plain black loading/tip screen with small gold text, a steep comedown from `gallery4/06_menu`'s composed archipelago main-menu shot, and because the reflections beneath that gorgeous opening shot still don't mirror any geometry up close.

## What's shippable vs. what's still holding it back

**Shippable now:** the opening cinematic, full stop (`01`, `13`) — the Round 4 stop-ship bug is verifiably closed; hull weathering contrast at gameplay zoom (`04`, `08`); sparse clustered whitecaps (`07`); the dark-disc cooldown timer (`04`); torn-lace explosion foam and ember splinter debris in the main fireball (`11`); island terrain strata (`01`, `12`); the Leviathan encounter and drone-swarm mothership silhouettes (`03`, `14`); age-choice cards (`05`).

**Still holding it back:** planar reflections that still don't mirror recognizable geometry under zoom, verified pixel-identical to Round 4's failing case; foliage that is textually claimed "higher-contrast" but crops the same as last round; HUD panel bodies still a flat gradient fill wearing new rivets instead of real baked material; a loading/menu screen that undersells the game relative to its own opening shot; a team-color hull rim light that is inconsistent — visible on some ships in calm water, essentially invisible once smoke and clutter arrive in a real brawl (`04`, `07`).

## Top 8 Remaining Fixes (ranked by impact)

1. **Reflections still don't mirror geometry — verified unchanged from Round 4.** Cropping the rock spire + waterline in `12_reflections` (x:150–450, y:300–620) against the identical crop from `gallery4/12_reflections` produces near-identical pixels: the water under the spire is still generic teal wave-noise with no darker mirrored rock silhouette, and no mirrored castle wall is visible in the water directly in front of it in `01`/`13`. Recipe: confirm the reflection pass renders actual mirrored scene geometry to an offscreen `WebGLRenderTarget` (a mirrored camera flipped through the water plane), not just an env-map/sky sample; composite with `mix(base, reflectionSample, fresnel)` where `fresnel = pow(1.0 - dot(N, V), 5.0)`; cut wave-normal-map distortion strength on the reflection UV lookup by ~60% so silhouettes survive instead of dissolving into noise.
2. **HUD panel body is still a flat fill — rivets were added, the material was not.** Crop the Armory panel bottom-right in any HUD shot (`02`, `04`, `07`, `08`, `09`, `10`, `14`): gold rivet dots now sit on the border, but the panel interior between border and text is a single smooth navy-to-black vertical gradient with zero grain, no brass highlight sweep, no wood pore texture. Recipe unchanged from two rounds ago: bake a `CanvasTexture` (riveted brass + dark walnut, normal+AO) once at load and sample it as the panel's diffuse/normal input, with a soft specular highlight (`pow(max(dot(R,V),0.0), 24.0) * 0.3`) sweeping across it.
3. **Foliage canopies are pixel-identical to Round 4 — the claimed contrast bump isn't visible.** In `10_island`, cropping the same tree cluster region used last round shows the same single lumpy "broccoli" sphere per tree, same mottled bump-map contrast, no per-cluster silhouette variance. Recipe (repeated from Round 4 fix #4, still outstanding): replace the single sphere per canopy with 6–10 randomly offset/rotated/scaled small blob meshes so the silhouette actually breaks up at the edges; that's a geometry change a texture-contrast tweak cannot substitute for.
4. **Cooldown icon glyph doesn't desaturate during recharge, contradicting the stated change.** In the `04_battle_closeup` Q/W/E crop, the dark disc + numeral is present and legible, but the icon line-art underneath (goggles/arrow glyphs) stays the same bright white on cooldown as it is when ready — only the numeral and a faint dark wedge change. Recipe: multiply the icon glyph's alpha/brightness by `mix(0.35, 1.0, 1.0 - cooldownFraction)` and drop saturation toward 0 via `mix(color, vec3(luminance), cooldownFraction*0.6)` so the ready/not-ready states are readable by silhouette brightness alone, without reading the numeral.
5. **`06_menu` undersells the game relative to its own opening cinematic.** It's a plain near-black frame with small centered gold serif text ("ARMADA ASCENSION — MUSTERING THE FLEETS") and one line of tip text — no archipelago, no ship, no light, unlike `gallery4/06_menu`'s composed main-menu shot with the same establishing-shot background. Recipe: reuse the Round-4-style main-menu composition (blurred/darkened archipelago render behind the text, per `gallery4/06_menu`) as the base for this loading/transition state too, instead of falling back to solid black.
6. **Team-color Fresnel rim light is real but disappears exactly when it's needed most.** It's faintly visible on the isolated red ship "Themistocles" in `03_leviathan` (crop x:280–500,y:60–170) in calm water, but in the actual brawls (`04_battle_closeup`, `07_mid`) where smoke and multiple hulls overlap, no rim glow survives — ships read as flat silhouettes again. Recipe: increase the rim intensity term itself (`pow(1.0-dot(N,V),2.0) * factionColor * 1.8`, up from what reads as ~0.6–0.8 now) and render it as an additive pass so it isn't multiplied down by the smoke/fog overlay sitting on top of the hull.
7. **Explosion debris is only ember-glowing inside the main fireball, not in ordinary hull-hit debris.** `11_explosion`'s core correctly throws glowing splinter chunks (confirmed by crop), but the small dark rectangular debris chunks flying off hulls during ordinary gunfire in `04_battle_closeup` (scattered around the center ship) are flat matte black/brown with no emissive tint at all. Recipe: give the debris-chunk shader a small constant emissive term (`emissive: 0xff6622, emissiveIntensity: 0.4` fading to 0 over the sprite's ~1s lifetime) everywhere debris spawns, not only in the death/explosion VFX path.
8. **Wake foam reads as thin veined cracks rather than clumps.** The claimed "broken clumps instead of a net" is a real shape change (crop of `04_battle_closeup`'s wake vs `gallery4` confirms a jagged, lightning-crack-like trail replacing the old grid), but it's thin and wispy at 1x zoom, closer to cracked ice than foam. Recipe: increase the point size / opacity floor of the foam particle clumps by ~30–40% and reduce the count of the thinnest trailing wisps so the remaining clumps read as solid whitecap chunks rather than hairline veins.

## Verdict

This round fixes the one thing that actually mattered most: the opening cinematic is no longer a coin-flip between "gorgeous" and "two-thirds black," and that alone should move the Wow score more than anything else could have. Real, verified craft gains also show up in hull weathering, terrain strata, the cooldown timer's dark-disc readability, sparse whitecaps, and torn-lace explosion foam with genuine ember debris — a team that measurably shipped what it said it would in several places. But three claims in this round's changelist don't survive a pixel-level check against last round's captures — reflections, leaf-clump contrast, and (functionally) the HUD panel material, which got rivets instead of the promised bevel and sheen — and a AAA studio's art director would flag "we said we fixed it and the crop is identical to last build" as a bigger process problem than any single shader bug. Ship the opening cinematic fix immediately; then spend the next round on reflections and real HUD material, which are now the two oldest unresolved line items in this review's history.
