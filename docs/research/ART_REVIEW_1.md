# Armada Ascension — Art Review 1

Reviewed: `tests/output/gallery/01_menu.png` → `10_island.png`. Rendered headless/SwiftShader; software-rasterizer noise and hint/announcement text overlap are ignored per brief.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 6/10.** The bones of a good identity are there — warm gold/navy nautical typography, a genuinely pretty low-angle ocean shader with sun glitter, and a coherent "Age of Sail → Airpower" fantasy. But it reads as an itch.io prototype, not a shipped title: the hero menu shot has a visibly broken/warped island (01_menu), every single island is a perfect compass-drawn circle of sand, trees are identical cauliflower blobs, and the signature explosion VFX (09_explosion) is a flat gradient sphere with no smoke silhouette. These are the details AAA art directors obsess over precisely because they're what separates "coded by a programmer" from "art directed."

**VFX Readability: 6/10.** The kill-banner system (FIRST BLOOD, DOUBLE SINK — 04_mid, 05_late, 08_dusk) is excellent: legible, well-kerned, correctly hierarchized (event / actor / sub-clause). That's real AAA UX. But the storm bloom (07_storm) is blown to pure white with no falloff so it reads as a UI error rather than a shell impact, the swarm (06_swarm) dissolves into a flat cloud of identical dots with no readable leader/direction, and at fleet-battle zoom (08_dusk) ships collapse into color-coded dashes indistinguishable from debug markers — you cannot tell what is fighting what.

**UI/UX: 7/10.** Information architecture is genuinely solid: score/timer top-center, ability bar + resource + portrait bottom-center, tech tree ("Armory") bottom-right, minimap bottom-left — that's a textbook MOBA HUD and nothing is missing. The weakness is skin, not layout: every panel is the same flat navy rectangle with a thin gold hairline, ability icons are unstyled line glyphs with no cooldown/rarity treatment, and the minimap is an unornamented dark rectangle that doesn't feel like it belongs to the same nautical brand as the logo.

## Top 12 Fixes (ranked by visual impact × feasibility)

1. **[01_menu]** The large background island is visibly warped/torn (mesh or UV distortion on the biggest landmass in the hero shot). Any AAA studio would consider this a P0 blocking bug before a single lighting pass. Fix: audit the terrain generator for NaN/seam errors at high subdivision, verify tangent-space normals aren't reading from a degenerate UV island, and add a regression screenshot test on the menu background specifically.
2. **[01,03,04,05,07,08,10]** Every island is a mathematically perfect circle of sand — the single most "generated, not designed" tell in the whole game. AAA coastal art (Sea of Thieves, Genshin's islands) always breaks the primitive silhouette. Fix: perturb island radius with 2–3 octaves of domain-warped fbm noise per angle (`r(θ) = r0 * (1 + 0.15*fbm(θ*3) + 0.06*fbm(θ*9))`), then run a 1-2 pass Voronoi relaxation on the shoreline vertices so no two islands share the same silhouette.
3. **[07_storm, 09_explosion]** Explosions/muzzle flashes clip to flat white with a hard-edged circular boundary — two overlapping "suns" with no energy falloff, which reads as an HDR/tonemap bug rather than an explosion. Fix: switch the post pipeline to ACES filmic tonemapping, drive bloom intensity from actual luminance (threshold ~1.0, soft knee), and shape the flash with an inverse-square radial falloff texture instead of a hard-alpha sprite.
4. **[01_menu, 10_island]** Trees are identical uniform "broccoli" instances — no size, color, or silhouette variance, which is the fastest way to make a forest look like `THREE.InstancedMesh` demo code. Fix: jitter each instance's scale (±20%), rotation, and foliage hue (vary a canvas-baked gradient's hue by ±8° and value by ±10% per instance seed), and mix in 2-3 silhouette variants (rounded, conical, wind-swept).
5. **[all in-game]** HUD panels are one repeated flat-navy-rectangle-with-gold-hairline component everywhere (score bar, ability bar, Armory, portrait, minimap frame) — reads as a UI kit, not art direction. Fix: bake a subtle canvas-generated brass/riveted-wood normal+AO texture for panel backgrounds, add a 2px inner bevel highlight + outer drop shadow, and vary corner ornamentation (compass-rose corner ticks) so panels feel forged rather than div'd.
6. **[01,03,04,05]** No shoreline foam or ship bow-wake detail at any island or hull — water just stops at the sand with a hard alpha edge. Fix: add a depth-difference-based soft-particle foam band at the coastline (`foam = saturate(1 - (sceneDepth - fragDepth)/foamWidth)`) and a scrolling wake normal-map trail behind each ship's stern using its velocity vector.
7. **[09_explosion]** The signature explosion is a single flat radial-gradient sphere with sparse dark speckle debris — no plume, no shockwave shaping, no secondary combustion. This is the moment players screenshot; it needs to earn that. Fix: layer 3 elements — (a) an additive core sprite with the fixed-falloff fix from #3, (b) a soft-particle smoke shell (3-4 billboards, dark-to-transparent, slow upward drift + turbulence via curl noise), (c) a screen-space refraction shockwave ring (distort UV by a ring-shaped displacement that expands and fades over ~0.4s).
8. **[03-08 progression shots]** Color grade is nearly identical cool teal/cyan across every game-age screenshot despite the fiction explicitly progressing Age of Sail → Steam → Airpower → Dreadnoughts. AAA naval/strategy titles use grade to sell tech progression for free. Fix: add a per-Age LUT/color-grade pass (Age of Sail: clean cool blue as-is; Age of Steam: warmer amber highlights + soot-grey shadows; Age of Airpower: desaturated stormy grey-blue with higher contrast; Age of Dreadnoughts: cold steel blue with harder specular).
9. **[all]** Ability icons (Q/W/E/R) are flat unstyled line glyphs with no cooldown feedback, no resource-color coding, and no depth. Fix: add a radial cooldown-wipe shader overlay (simple `discard` on angle > progress), a colored inner glow keyed to resource type (kinetic=gold, utility=blue), and a 1px emboss so icons sit "in" the socket rather than floating on top.
10. **[08_dusk]** At full fleet-battle zoom, every ship collapses to a plain colored dash — total loss of ship silhouette (no masts/funnels/turrets), indistinguishable from a debug overlay. Fix: give the far-LOD billboard a baked ship-class silhouette icon (3-4 archetypes: brawler/assassin/artillery/swarm) instead of a rectangle, colored by faction, so players read formation shape at a glance.
11. **[06_swarm]** Drone swarm is a cloud of identical flat white dots with no leader read, trail, or depth cue. Fix: give each drone a short additive motion-trail (2-3 frame history alpha falloff), vary scale/rotation per-instance, and bias opacity/size upward for drones near the front of the formation so the swarm reads as a directed mass, not noise.
12. **[all]** Minimap is a bare unstyled rectangle with plain dots — no fog-of-war edge, no radar sweep, disconnected from the nautical brand. Fix: mask the minimap with a soft vignette + parchment-brown border matching the HUD panel material from #5, and add a slow rotating radar-sweep alpha wedge over the fog-of-war layer for ambient motion.

## Five things that read as "procedural/cheap/LLM-made" (and how to hide them)

1. **Perfect-circle islands (every screenshot).** Fixed by #2 above — this is the single biggest tell and the highest-leverage fix in the whole review.
2. **Uniform cauliflower tree instancing (01, 10).** Fixed by #4 — variance is nearly free once the instancing loop exists.
3. **Flat radial-gradient explosion (09).** A gradient gizmo, not a combustion event. Fixed by #7.
4. **One reused flat-panel UI component for every HUD element (all).** The tell is that the score bar, ability bar, Armory, and portrait frame are visually the *same rectangle* at different sizes. Fixed by #5 — even one material variation (metal vs. wood vs. parchment) per panel type kills the "it's all one `<div>`" impression.
5. **Zero color-grade variance across the entire tech-tree progression (03-08).** A game literally named "Ascension" that doesn't visually escalate is leaving its best free win on the table. Fixed by #8.

## Three things that already work and should not be touched

1. **The kill/event banner typography system** (FIRST BLOOD, DOUBLE SINK, "X advanced to the Age of Y" — 04, 05, 08). Serif display face, gold hairline rule, small-caps subtext, correctly prioritized event feed on the right — this is shipped-MOBA quality exactly as-is. Don't restyle it while fixing everything else.
2. **The menu ocean shader's specular sun-glitter and depth-based color falloff (01_menu).** Independent of the geometry bug, the water color grade (deep teal → warm horizon glow) and the sparkle pattern are genuinely attractive and worth reusing as the base for in-match water once foam/wake are added.
3. **HUD information architecture / layout** (score-timer-score top-center, ability+resource+portrait bottom-center, tech tree bottom-right, minimap bottom-left, all screenshots). The *placement* is correct MOBA convention and highly legible — only the *material/skin* of these panels needs work (see fix #5), not their position or hierarchy.
