# Armada Ascension - Art Review 19

Scope: 35 gallery18 shots plus options.png, end_win.png, end_lose.png, compat_then_medium.png, karst_a.png, port_a.png (karst_b is a near-dup of karst_a, port_a of 32). Honest read: the cinematic stills (00, 01, 13, 18, 22) are the strongest material and are genuinely good. The gameplay camera (07, 19, 20, 17, 04) is where it still reads as procedural/indie.

## Scores

| Area | Score |
|---|---|
| Visual Quality (environment, ships, water) | 7.5 |
| VFX | 7 |
| UI/UX and controls clarity | 7 |
| First-10-Seconds Wow | 8 |
| Camera readability | 6.5 |
| Polish consistency | 6.5 |

**Visual Quality 7.5.** Water (reflection, sun glitter, teal depth gradient; 00, 12, 22) is the best asset and holds up. Crimson fort (22, 18) is a real silhouette with warm window glow. Karst has strata banding and a moss cap but still has the same few rounded "thumb" profiles repeated everywhere (00 right side, 22, 30): they read as stacked cylinders, and the sine strata (visible as wavy contour lines, 00 x1180-1340, y200-500) looks like a displacement-noise texture, not rock. The Azure castle (01, 12, 19) is the weakest hero asset: uniform brick texture at one scale, saturated blue cones, flat sandy colour. It reads as a theme-park/Lego castle next to the nicely designed Crimson pagoda fort; the two factions are not the same quality tier. Ships: sail ships (04, 07) good silhouettes; Dreadnoughts (08) are small, low-contrast blue-grey, and similar to each other; Drone Mothership (23, 14) is a plain faceted slab, the flattest model in the game. Whales (26) are unlit black slabs with no surface detail.

**VFX 7.** Hit/explosion in 25/34/11/21 is hot and readable; Leviathan (03) is a standout; storm rain (09) is clean. Problems: shock rings are thin hard-edged vector-looking ellipses (21, 18, 19, 03 reading as UI circles, not water displacement); the white "snowflake" foam stars scattered across open water (19 x40-760 y250-650, 20 x370-1250 y280-640) look like sprite stamps and sit at the same size/alpha; the ability dome (05/23) is a clean hex shield but identical for every ship; explosion smoke in 21 is a hard-edged polygon silhouette (x520-740 y200-430 looks like a hexagon billboard); sparks in 07/04 are uniform round dots. The crippled-captain smoke tell (34) is still not distinguishable.

**UI/UX 7.** Consistent gold-brass-on-navy theme, good type pairing (serif headings, sans body), strong menu (06) and result screens (end_win/end_lose). But: (1) the HUD is a stack of five equally weighted boxes with the same border - no hierarchy; (2) ship name truncates "Ship-of-the-Line F..." in the unit card (04, 07, 08 x430-650 y800-815) and the level numeral collides with the title; (3) ability slots use abstract icons (glasses, chevrons, funnel) with no label, so ability meaning is opaque until hover; cooldown numerals are tiny/grey on the dark slot (07 x805 y838); (4) the "Armory" panel has no cost shown until gold is high (04 vs 07) so the layout jumps (panel height changes between 04 and 08); (5) the unit card shows a 3D thumbnail that is cropped mid-hull (08 x420-665 y700-780); (6) the options panel (options.png) is the only elegant dialog but its graphics description text is default grey 14px and sliders are default-looking browser ranges with gold fill; the gear icon at top right (options.png x1200 y30) is a different language to the "?" button; (7) end screen shows "AZURE RAZED THE ENEMY CITADEL . 5:32" identically in win and lose, and "You (you)" is redundant; the scoreboard is dense plain table with no portraits/medals; (8) 1280x650 (24, options) clips LEVEL callouts per Review 18 and compresses the armory into 2-line rows; (9) Menu title/rule alignment is great but the left half is a dark vignette over empty water - the 3D scene behind it is mostly god-rays + glitter, not ships.

**First-10-Seconds Wow 8.** Opening enemy reveal (00) and cinematic push-in (01) are cinema-grade: low sun, fog, god-ray, reflections. It collapses the moment gameplay starts: 02 is a high 3/4 view with a blown-out god-ray column through the ship and a bubble-ring flower; then 04 shows an almost empty teal plain with reflections of karst turned into blurry teal blobs. The transition from "film" to "game" is a visible downgrade in composition.

**Camera 6.5.** Gameplay camera is too high and too tight to the player to read the lane/armada: enemy nameplates stack as unreadable pills (16: Togo/Tromp/Ching Shih health bars overlap hull, x580-1080 y115-150; 17 same), health bars float far above masts so you cannot tell which bar belongs to which hull, and big props (crane in 17, 23, 04 foreground rooftops) occlude the ship the player controls. Reflections of tall karst below the horizon (07, 04, 26) crowd the foreground with dark teal triangles that are as visually loud as ships. Low-angle brawl (16) is wonderful for screenshots and bad for play. Zoomed-in (17) shows the player ship partially covered by a port crane.

**Polish consistency 6.5.** Three different rendering "languages" coexist: photoreal-ish water/sky, hand-modeled low-poly props (port, trees), and flat/texture-light vessel hulls (Mothership, Dreadnought, whales). Vegetation (10) is a clear step down: repeated identical canopy blob, uniform scale, hard flat-green ground with no grass/rock/dirt breakup. Bloom bleeds fringing on high-contrast edges (16 top, 07 left). The shore foam ring is a uniform bright white band (08, 10, 16) with no variation. Typography and gold theme are the single most consistent layer.

## What stops this being a 10 - top 12 by impact per hour

1. **Camera framing: pull back/tilt for readability and lift the HUD plates off the action.** Shots 04, 07, 17, 16 (full frame). Raise default distance 15-20 percent, cap pitch, add a soft occluder fade for props between camera and player ship (crane in 17 x540-1040 y280-800). `src/core/camera.js`.
2. **Tame the reflection of tall karst into foreground water.** 04 y0-250, 07 y0-250, 26 y230-400: huge dark teal triangles compete with ships. Fade reflection by distance/pitch and tint closer to sky so it reads as soft ghost. `src/render/reflection.js` plus ocean shader `src/render/ocean.js`.
3. **Nameplate/health bar layout.** 16 x380-1080 y110-150, 17 x370-520 y105-215, 08/19 enemy bars floating at map-top: stack collisions and bars detached from hulls. Declutter (cap per cluster, collapse to chevron icons beyond N, anchor to hull top not mast top). `src/ui/hud.js` (nameplate pass ~line 226 and 651).
4. **Replace the white snowflake foam stamps on open water.** 19 and 20 across the whole mid-frame, 06 x620-780 y400-600 (cotton-blob splashes). Vary size/alpha, age-fade, align to wave normal, or drop; they flag "sprite" instantly. `src/render/wakes.js` (dotted foam) and `src/render/particles.js` kind 3 (spray).
5. **Rebuild the Azure castle to the Crimson fort's quality tier.** 01, 12, 19, 20 (castle body). Needs scale variation in brickwork, slate/dark tile roofs instead of saturated blue cones (the blue cones make the base look like a toy), banners, walls with thickness, weathering/streaks. `src/render/models/structureModels.js` (roundTower/coneRoof ~lines 172-179, 408) and `src/render/models/materials.js`.
6. **Ability shock rings and hard circles read as vector UI.** 21 and 18 (orange ring x290-1000 y180-640, x100-1500 y470-770), 03 (x520 y620, x810 y710 hollow circles), 05/20 (cyan loop). Add ring thickness gradient, wave-distortion, soft inner glow, noise break-up. `src/render/fx.js` ringMat lines 13-30.
7. **Truncated ship name and card layout.** 04/07/08/12 unit card text "Ship-of-the-Line F..." plus level "1" overlapping at x640-655 y800-815. Use short display name or two-line wrap, move level to a badge. `src/ui/hud.js`, `src/ui/style.css`.
8. **Ability bar clarity.** 07 x690-1000 y800-870: icons without labels, cooldown numerals tiny; add name tooltip on first use or short caption under key, ring-fill cooldown with text 18px, clear "ready" pulse. `src/ui/hud.js` + `src/ui/icons.js` (37 lines - only a few glyphs, several generic).
9. **Distinct modelling for late-age hulls and whales.** 14/23 (Drone Mothership flat faceted slab), 08 (dreadnoughts small and similar), 26 (black whales). Add panel lines, deck clutter, rust/weathering streaks, belly/throat lighter colour for whales. `src/render/models/shipModels.js`, `src/render/sealife.js`, `src/render/weathering.js`.
10. **Karst variety and cliff texturing.** 00 x1180-1340 y200-520, 22 x1130-1360 y150-450, 30: wavy contour lines look like noise, silhouettes are rounded thumbs; add overhangs, notches, vertical fluting, scree skirts and waterline undercut/notch. `src/render/environment.js` karstGeometry (line 260) and karstTower (292).
11. **Tone down god-ray column and bloom in gameplay start.** 02 x780-840 y60-720 vertical beam through ship; 06 menu beams are pretty but the same beam in 02 hides the first ship and washes the UI. Gate to cinematic only. `src/render/fx.js` (~line 329) and `src/render/renderer.js` (post).
12. **Result/menu/options UI polish.** end_win/end_lose: same subtitle text, no per-team icons, plain table; options.png: default-looking sliders, gear/"?" mismatch, description text; 24/options at 1280x650 clip. Add faction crests, MVP portrait, row highlights for the player, custom range styling, resolve the clipping LEVEL banner. `src/ui/style.css`, `src/ui/hud.js`, `src/main.js`.

## Smaller notes
- Empty areas: 04, 07, 20 mid-frame is open teal with nothing but wake; add sparse flotsam, seabirds near islands, ripples, kelp patches, drifting fog banks. `src/render/environment.js`, `src/render/birds.js`.
- Flat lighting: 10 island is evenly lit olive with no sun/shadow break (forest floor reads as one value); 14 and 23 mothership is uniformly dark blue; add cascade-lit shadow and rim. `src/render/renderer.js`, `src/render/cloudShadow.js`.
- Tiling: trees in 10 and 30 repeat an identical canopy; port buildings in 32 and 23 reuse the same three house shapes; karst reflection stripes repeat in 12.
- Weak silhouettes: Dreadnought at distance in 18/22/08 loses its outline against teal; add team-coloured rim (see `src/render/teamRim.js`) at range.
- Missing feedback: no hit marker or directional damage indicator in 34/16 other than numbers; sinking state in 29 reads, but "crippled" tell does not; ability on cooldown gives no on-world cue.
