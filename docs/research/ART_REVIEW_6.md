# Armada Ascension — Art Review 6

Reviewed every image in `tests/output/gallery6/` against `gallery5` and the Round 5 write-up, with pixel-level crops (`PIL`, 2–8x zoom, some brightness-boosted) on reflections, HUD panels, foliage, debris, wakes and rim light rather than eyeballing full frames alone. SwiftShader noise, harness title cards, stale hints and "SUNK" overlays ignored per brief; the letterbox black in `01`/`12`/`13` is intentional and ignored.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 8.3/10 (+0.5).** Two of Round 5's oldest unresolved defects are now genuinely fixed. Cropping the small watchtower reflection in `12_reflections` (x:150–500,y:400–650) shows the mirrored tower now reads in its own tan/brown stone color with a blue roof, where the identical crop in `gallery5` showed only generic teal wave-noise under it — the energy-conserving Fresnel change is real and verified. The Armory HUD panel body (`04_battle_closeup`, `02_reforging` crops) now carries visible brushed-metal grain and a diagonal warm sheen band in the lower-right corner, replacing three rounds of flat gradient fill. Foliage lobes (`10_island`) are a smaller win than claimed: a few trees do show new pointed leaf-lobes breaking the silhouette (e.g. the cluster around x:550–650,y:150–250), but most canopies are still a single round "broccoli" mass — the geometry change is present but under-applied.

**VFX Readability: 9.0/10 (+0.1).** Debris ember glow is only weakly verified: the dark rectangular hull-debris chunk in `04_battle_closeup` (x:1250–1320,y:260–320) does show a faint warm maroon tint at its edge under brightening, but it sits directly beside the fireball, so this could be bloom bleed rather than an intrinsic emissive term — it needs testing on debris far from any fire to be sure. Wake foam in `09_storm` shows the near-ship wake staying solid and dense while the trailing far end frays and gaps, consistent with the claimed young/old split, though the effect is subtle at 1x. Everything from Round 5 (torn-lace explosion ring, sparse whitecaps, dark-disc cooldown timer) still holds up.

**UI/UX: 9.3/10 (+0.3).** The HUD panel material fix (see above) closes a defect this review has flagged for three straight rounds — genuine, verified progress, not a cosmetic dodge. But a new inconsistency appeared: the Age V boss banner in `03_leviathan` mixes two unrelated type systems in the same stack — "DOMINATING" and "YAMAMOTO IS ON A RAMPAGE" in the elegant serif/small-caps used everywhere else, directly above "THE LEVIATHAN" in a chunky black-outlined game-UI bitmap font that looks like a placeholder. Cooldown icons (`04_battle_closeup` W/E crop) still don't desaturate on cooldown — Round 5 fix #4 remains unaddressed.

**First-10-Seconds Wow Factor: 8.8/10 (+0.8).** `06_menu` is transformed: Round 5's plain black loading screen is gone, replaced by a fully composed main menu — logo lockup, fleet/difficulty/graphics selectors, "SET SAIL" CTA, all laid over a live, in-focus showreel battle (light-beam capture points, ship wakes, islands) of the same quality as the opening cinematic. That was this review's #5 fix last round and it landed cleanly. `01`/`13` opening cinematics remain rock solid.

## What's shippable vs. what's still holding it back

**Shippable now:** the opening cinematic (`01`, `13`); the new main menu (`06`) — a first-impression frame that finally matches the game's own best shot; HUD panel material with baked grain and sheen (`02`, `04`, `07`–`10`, `14`); reflections that now carry mirrored-object color instead of generic teal (`12`, verified by crop); hull weathering, terrain strata, torn-lace explosions, sparse whitecaps (all confirmed unchanged-good from Round 5); age-choice cards (`05`); Leviathan and drone-swarm mothership silhouettes (`03`, `14`).

**Still holding it back:** foliage canopies that are still fundamentally round blobs with only a few trees showing the promised extra lobes; a boss-kill banner that suddenly drops into a mismatched placeholder-looking bitmap font (`03`); cooldown ability icons that stay full-brightness on cooldown instead of dimming/desaturating; debris ember glow that isn't clearly distinguishable from nearby fireball bloom.

## Top 8 Remaining Fixes (ranked by impact)

1. **Boss/killstreak banner mixes two unrelated type systems in one stack.** In `03_leviathan` (crop x:650–950,y:180–300), "DOMINATING" and "YAMAMOTO IS ON A RAMPAGE" render in the game's elegant serif/small-caps, but "THE LEVIATHAN" directly beneath renders in a chunky sans-serif with a hard black outline — it looks like an unstyled placeholder dropped into a finished UI. Recipe: route the boss name-plate through the same `<CanvasTexture>`/font-atlas path used for the kill-feed and banner text (same serif family, same drop-shadow/glow treatment, no black stroke outline), just at a different size/color for hierarchy.
2. **Foliage lobe fix is real but under-applied — most canopies are still single round blobs.** In `10_island` (crop x:300–750,y:80–420), only 2–3 trees out of ~20 in frame show a new pointed outer lobe; the rest are unchanged from `gallery5`. Recipe: the lobe-instancing code exists (confirmed by the trees that do show it) — raise its application rate/probability to ~100% of canopies and randomize lobe count 3–5 and scale 0.4–0.7x of the main canopy radius so it isn't a rare accent but the default silhouette.
3. **Debris ember glow can't be told apart from fireball bloom.** The one hull-debris chunk inspected near the `04_battle_closeup` fireball (x:1250–1320,y:260–320) shows a warm tint only on the side facing the fire. Recipe: add an explicit small constant `emissiveIntensity` (≈0.3, color `0xff6622`) to the debris chunk material so it self-illuminates identically regardless of distance from other light sources, and spot-check a chunk drifting away from any fire to confirm it still glows.
4. **Cooldown icon glyphs still don't desaturate — Round 5 fix #4 unaddressed.** In the `04_battle_closeup` Q/W/E crop, the W (chevrons) and E (arrow) glyphs render at full white brightness under the dark radial wipe; only the numeral communicates "not ready." Recipe (repeated): `alpha *= mix(0.35, 1.0, 1.0 - cooldownFraction)` and desaturate toward luminance by up to 60% while on cooldown.
5. **HUD sheen band is a static bake, not a light effect.** The diagonal lighter stripe now visible on the Armory panel (`04`, `07`–`10` crops) sits in the exact same screen-space position in every frame regardless of camera or time — it reads as a smudge on the texture rather than a specular highlight. Recipe: keep the baked grain/AO as diffuse, but drive the sheen band's position with a UV offset uniform tied to elapsed time (`sheenPos = fract(uTime * 0.05)`) so it visibly sweeps every several seconds, the way a real brushed-metal panel catches light.
6. **Reflection fix is verified but only tested on a small isolated object — check it holds on the main castle.** The watchtower crop (`12_reflections`, x:150–500,y:400–650) clearly shows correct tan/brown mirrored color, but the large central castle's own reflection (directly under it, x:400–950,y:650–850) is inconclusive at this resolution — it's plausible the same fix applies, but it hasn't been confirmed on the hero asset itself. Recipe: capture a dedicated top-down or lower-camera shot centered on the castle's own waterline and diff it against `gallery5`'s equivalent to close this out definitively.
7. **Wake young/old contrast is present but too subtle to read as a deliberate design choice.** The `09_storm` wake crop shows the near-ship end slightly denser than the far end, but at normal battle-cam zoom the whole trail still reads as one continuous foam ribbon. Recipe: widen the opacity gradient — near-ship foam alpha ~0.9, far-end (>3s old) alpha ~0.35 — so the aging effect is legible without needing a crop to see it.
8. **Team rim light couldn't be confirmed or denied this round.** Round 5 flagged it as invisible in real brawls; this round's autopilot captures (`04`, `07`, `08`) put the player's own ship and allies in positions where hull edges are either off-frame or already team-colored (making the rim hard to isolate from base paint). Recipe: add a fixed debug camera angle to the test harness — one ship, side-lit, half in smoke — so this specific claim can be verified pixel-for-pixel in Round 7 instead of staying an open question for a second round running.

## Verdict

This is the strongest round since the opening-cinematic fix two rounds ago, because the team closed two multi-round-old debts at once: the HUD panel finally has a real baked material instead of decorated flatness, and the main menu is no longer an embarrassment sitting one click behind a gorgeous cinematic — both verified by crop, not taken on faith. The reflection energy-conservation fix is real where it was tested (a small isolated tower) but the review couldn't yet confirm it on the hero castle reflection itself, and the foliage-lobe and debris-ember claims are both technically true but so lightly applied that a player won't notice them in motion. A new, avoidable defect — a placeholder-looking bitmap font on the Leviathan kill banner — is exactly the kind of inconsistency that breaks the "$100M studio" illusion the rest of the frame is earning. Ship the menu and HUD-material wins immediately; spend Round 7 finishing what Round 6 started (foliage coverage, debris emissive, the banner font) rather than opening new fronts.
