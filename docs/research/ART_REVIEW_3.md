# Armada Ascension — Art Review 3

Reviewed: `tests/output/gallery3/01_opening_cinematic.png` → `11_explosion.png` against Round 2 (`gallery2`). SwiftShader software-rasterizer noise, SUNK overlays, and stale hint/announcement text ignored per brief.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 8/10 (+0).** Real gains — the age-choice ship thumbnails (05) and HUD portrait render are genuinely AAA-card quality, the Leviathan boss (03) is a strong new silhouette, and the menu's multi-lighthouse archipelago (06) reads as art-directed, not generated. But this round also confirms Round 1's oldest unresolved bug at the worst location: the opening cinematic (01) — the first frame a player sees — is blown to near-total white across half the composition, the same hard-clipped "HDR bug" flagged in Round 1 fix #3 and only "partially fixed" in Round 2. Three rounds in, this should be a P0. Net: strong new assets, offset by a marquee-shot regression of an old defect.

**VFX Readability: 7.5/10 (+0.5).** The Round 2-proposed shockwave ring on explosions is implemented and reads correctly (11_explosion has a real expanding energy ring, not just smoke+core). The Reforging hex-shield dome and light pillar (02) is a genuine spectacle moment. But the storm-squall explosion (09) still shows the identical twin-blown-out-circle clipping from Round 1 — unfixed, verified at scale exactly as Round 2 worried — and fleet-zoom ship silhouettes (07, 08) are still indistinguishable colored blobs, so Round 1 fix #10 remains open after three passes.

**UI/UX: 8.5/10 (+0.5).** The rendered ship thumbnails inside the age-choice cards (05) are the clearest win in this build — proper 3D renders with soft vignette and consistent lighting, on par with a tech-tree card in a shipped 4X/strategy title. Kill-feed and objective-capture messaging continue to read cleanly. Still capped: the ability bar shows raw cooldown *numbers* ("10", "17") with no radial wipe or resource-color coding (Round 2 fix #5, still open), and HUD panel bodies remain flat fills with no confirmable baked material (Round 2 fix #1, unverifiable again).

**First-10-Seconds Wow Factor: 7/10 (new metric).** The letterboxed sunrise cinematic, silhouetted fleet, and castle establishing shot are the right instinct — a real "trailer open." It's undercut by the same bloom-clipping defect eating the sky, and a castle/mountain backdrop reading flatter/matte than the water beneath it. The bones of a good open are here; the tonemap bug is the only thing between this and a genuinely strong first impression.

## What's now "shippable AAA-indie" vs. what's still holding it back

**Shippable now:** the age-choice/portrait ship thumbnails (05); the Reforging hex-dome + light-pillar hero moment (02), which is close to the exact recombination recipe proposed in Review 2; the explosion shockwave ring (11); the menu's lighthouse-archipelago composition (06); the Leviathan's overall silhouette and encounter framing (03).

**Still holding it back:** the sun-disc/explosion tonemap clipping that has survived three rounds and just appeared in the opening cinematic; flat, scale-less tree canopies at close range (10) despite Round 2 marking tree variance "fixed" — it holds at distance but collapses in a close hero shot; a wake ribbon (04) that is still a static geometric decal, not a turbulent foam-textured trail; a Leviathan body that is a smooth, unscaled green tube; unreadable ship-class silhouettes at mid/fleet zoom; and out-of-place flat pink cone props in the 10_island forest reading as placeholder assets.

## Top 8 Remaining Fixes (ranked)

1. **Fix the sun/explosion tonemap clipping globally, not per-effect (01, 06, 09).** This is the same defect from Round 1 fix #3, still open. Recipe: set `renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping` at the renderer level (not per-material), cap any emissive/sun-disc material color below ~2.0 pre-bloom, and drive `UnrealBloomPass` off actual scene luminance with `threshold: 0.85, strength: 0.6, radius: 0.4` instead of raw additive sprites — this one pipeline change fixes 01, 06, and 09 simultaneously instead of needing a bespoke fix per VFX.
2. **Ship-class silhouette LOD at fleet zoom (07, 08).** Ships are still colored blobs at battle-wide zoom — Round 1 fix #10, three rounds open. Recipe: swap the mesh for a baked `THREE.Sprite` billboard (4 archetype icons: brawler/assassin/artillery/carrier) once camera distance exceeds a threshold, tinted by faction via a shader uniform, so formations read at a glance.
3. **Turbulent, foam-textured ship wake (04).** Current wake is a flat, uniform-width decal. Recipe: drive UV offset of a scrolling foam normal map by `shipVelocity.length()`, erode the trailing edge with a scrolling Worley/curl-noise alpha mask (`alpha *= smoothstep(0.2, 0.6, noise(uv*8.0 - time))`), and taper width by trail age so it thins and scatters rather than ending in a hard clip.
4. **Leviathan skin detail (03).** Body reads as a smooth green tube. Recipe: add a triplanar scale/plate normal map, a fresnel rim-light term (`pow(1.0-dot(normal,viewDir), 3.0)`) tinted toward the bioluminescent head color, and vary segment hue slightly along the spline so the coils don't look like repeated primitives.
5. **Radial cooldown-wipe ability icons (still open from Review 2 #5).** Numeric-only cooldowns force players to read digits mid-combat. Recipe: layer a fragment-shader radial mask on the existing icon quad — `if (atan(uv.y-0.5, uv.x-0.5) + PI > progress * TWO_PI) discard;` — tinted by resource type (kinetic=gold, utility=blue).
6. **Storm rain as instanced particles, not static streak lines (09).** Current rain is uniform repeating diagonal lines with no variation or water interaction. Recipe: replace with an `InstancedMesh` of thin billboard streaks, randomized per-instance speed/length/opacity, plus a lightweight ripple decal spawned on a timer at random surface points near the camera.
7. **Bake real material into HUD panel bodies (still open from Review 2 #1).** Recipe unchanged and still unaddressed: canvas-bake a brass-hairline/riveted-wood normal+AO texture once at load and sample it as a `CanvasTexture` behind the existing gold corner-bracket ornament on score bar, ability bar, Armory, and portrait frame.
8. **Close-range forest pass (10).** Trees still read as blob canopies at hero distance, and the scattered flat pink cone props look unfinished. Recipe: at LOD-near distance, swap canopy geometry for a 2-3 triangle-fan cluster with a baked leaf-alpha texture instead of a single sphere, and either texture the pink cone props as driftwood/dead-tree stumps (add bark normal + desaturate) or cut them until they're dressed.

## Verdict

A sharp viewer would say this is a talented small team closing in fast, not yet a funded AAA studio — the age-choice cards, the Reforging hero moment, and the Leviathan encounter design would not look out of place in a funded indie darling, but the same hard-clipped bloom defect surviving three consecutive review rounds and landing in the opening cinematic is the kind of unpolished detail a shipped AAA title's QA pass would never let through. The single change that would move the needle most: fix the tonemapping pipeline once, globally, at the renderer level — it is one engineering change that simultaneously repairs the opening cinematic, the storm climax, and every future explosion, and it is the last visible "programmer art" tell standing between this build and a genuinely convincing funded-studio impression.

Note: HUD grain material and seabird flocks called out in the brief were not clearly verifiable in this 11-shot set (too subtle at these distances, or absent from these frames) — worth a dedicated close-up capture next round. Camera framing (letterboxed cinematic, the Reforging dolly-in) reads as intentional and works; no fix needed there.
