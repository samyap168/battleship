# Armada Ascension — Gameplay Review 1

Reviewed from code/docs only (no live playtest): `docs/GDD.md`, `src/core/config.js`, `src/game/{game,combat,units,abilities,drones,leviathan,map}.js`, `src/game/ai/bot.js`, `src/main.js`, `src/ui/hud.js`. Sim data: 11 bot-vs-bot matches, kills ranging 10–48 per side, 8/11 hitting the 600s cap.

## Scores

**Moment-to-moment Feel — 7/10.** Shells lead the target with distance-scaled scatter and real flight time (`combat.js:245-251`), turn rate drops with hull size (`config.js` `turn: 1.6→1.15→1.3`), and hold-QWER-to-aim/release-to-fire (`main.js:277-284,294-298`) gives skillshots real input weight. But the squall's only mechanical effect is widening `hero` gun scatter (`combat.js:248`); it doesn't touch creep or tower fire (`fireCreep`/`fireStructure` never read `G.storm`) or vision, so the GDD's "natural moment for ambushes" (GDD.md:99) isn't actually true in play — towers and lane creeps snipe through it unchanged.

**Strategic Depth — 6/10.** Independent hero aging vs. team-median era (`game.js:208-216`), trade ports, the Leviathan, and 5 layered comeback systems give real macro decisions. But the age-branch choice (`hud.js:173-186`) shows stats/abilities with zero counter-matchup guidance, and bots pick their branch with a coin flip (`bot.js:14` `branch = Math.random()<0.5?0:1`) with no counter-pick logic against the enemy comp — so branch choice reads as flavor more than strategy for both bots and a new player.

**Ability/Hull Design — 5/10.** 32 abilities cover distinct verbs (dash-stun, smoke, mines, homing, beam, swarm), but power is uneven: Dreadnought/Battleship's basic Q (`mainbattery`/`mainbattery2`, `config.js:136-138,160-162`) puts up to 900–1230 damage on an 8s cooldown with no `minLevel` gate, more raw burst than most hulls' actual ultimates on 45-55s cooldowns. Arsenal Cruiser's `railgun` (`config.js:183-184`) is a 390-damage, zero-travel-time, multi-target pierce beam on a 7s cooldown, also un-gated. Meanwhile Carrier's basic gun (`kind:'flak'`, `config.js:88`) behaves identically to every other gun kind in `combat.js` — the "anti-air specialist" flavor exists only in the name.

**Pacing & Balance — 5/10.** The 10-minute arc (waves, age costs, storm, Leviathan at 5:30) is well-scheduled and matches the sim data (median match reaches the cap). But the kill spread is wide even with 5+ comeback systems stacked (shutdown bounty, diminishing returns, kill-gold scaling, catch-up XP/gold, faster respawn, home waters, harbour repair) — 48-24 and 37-29 blowouts alongside 15-16 nail-biters suggest early snowball from the overtuned Q abilities above outruns the comeback math before it engages.

**Bot AI — 6/10.** The utility-AI (retreat/fight/capture/lane scoring, kiting at 85% gun range, `bot.js:59-76`) is a believable approximation of a human laner and reacts to being outnumbered, tower cover, and creep aggro. It falls short of "exploiting mistakes": `tryAbilities` (`bot.js:202-256`) tries its 4 abilities in fixed slot order with no CC-before-burst sequencing, so bots never intentionally stun-into-nuke — the single highest-skill-expression play in the kit is never used by the AI.

**Onboarding — 6/10.** Progressive hints at 9/26/48/80/120s (`game.js:417-425`) teach movement, dodging, last-hits, ports, and siege in the right order, and the age-up prompt fires the moment gold allows it (`game.js:428-432`). The gap: no hint ever states the hold-to-aim/release-to-fire skillshot mechanic in words — a first-time player discovers it only by holding Q and seeing `hud.js`'s aim cone appear.

## Top 10 improvements (ranked by impact)

1. **Nerf `mainbattery`/`mainbattery2` (`config.js:136-138,160-162`).** Raise `cd` from 8 to 14-16, or cut `count` from 6 to 4 and `dmg` by ~20%, or add a `minLevel: 3` gate. As written, Dreadnought/Battleship's basic Q out-damages most hulls' actual ultimates on a fraction of the cooldown, which is the likeliest driver of the 48-24/37-29 blowouts in the sim data.
2. **Nerf or telegraph `railgun` (`config.js:183-184`).** A 390-dmg, instant-hit, multi-target pierce beam on a 7s CD with no `minLevel` is the strongest DPS tool in the game per second of cooldown; cut `dmg` to ~260-300 or give it a 0.3-0.4s wind-up so it can be dodged like every other ability.
3. **Give the squall real teeth (`combat.js:248`, `game.js:408-411`).** Extend the scatter penalty to `fireCreep`/`fireStructure`, or add a vision-radius/target-acquisition-range cut for both teams. Currently only hero auto-guns are affected, so towers and creep lines punish exactly the ambushes the storm is supposed to enable.
4. **Sequence bot ability casts by role, not slot index (`bot.js:202-256`).** Add a pass before the `switch` that prefers `dash`/CC types (`stun`, `slow`) when `prey` is above ~60% hp and burst types when `prey` is low, so bots start comboing stun-into-nuke instead of firing abilities in fixed Q→R order.
5. **Fix the branch pick to counter-pick, not coin-flip (`bot.js:14,262-268`).** `this.branch = Math.random()<0.5?0:1` never looks at the enemy comp; weight it by what the enemy's median hull/role is (e.g., prefer Carrier/Torpedo vs. a Dreadnought-heavy enemy) so age-branch choice becomes a real strategic layer instead of a coin flip mirrored by every bot on the team.
6. **Differentiate Carrier's basic gun (`config.js:88`, `combat.js` `VIS.flak`).** Give `kind:'flak'` a real anti-drone/anti-missile tick in `fireGuns` (small bonus vs. `drone`/homing types it targets) so the Carrier's identity as an anti-air controller shows up outside its `pointdefense` ability.
7. **Add counter-matchup text to the age-choice modal (`hud.js:173-186`).** One line per card ("strong vs. slow artillery, weak to swarms") turns the biggest strategic decision in the match (Age III/IV/V branch) into a readable choice instead of a stat-sheet comparison for new players.
8. **Teach hold-to-aim explicitly (`game.js:417-423`).** Insert a hint the first time the player's cursor hovers a `target:'dir'`/`'point'` ability icon (or right after hint #2 at 26s): "Hold <b>Q/W/E/R</b> to aim, release to fire" — the mechanic is currently only discoverable by accident.
9. **Sync `REWARDS.heroGold`/`heroGoldPerAge` with the GDD (`config.js:237` vs. `GDD.md:65`).** Config pays 190+50×age per kill; the design doc says 240+80×age. Whichever is correct, the other is now actively misleading anyone tuning off the doc.
10. **Give Torpedo Cruiser a compensating buff if #1 ships (`config.js:70-76` vs. `63-69`).** Once Dreadnought's `mainbattery` is brought in line, Torpedo Cruiser (lower hp/armor, no shield/utility ability) needs its `torpspread` damage or `wolfpack` count nudged up so the Artillery/Assassin choice at Age III stays a real trade-off rather than Dreadnought-then-Battleship becoming the by-default line.
