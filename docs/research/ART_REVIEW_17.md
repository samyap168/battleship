# Armada Ascension — Art Review 17

All 30 shots reviewed at pixel level; the two Review-16 complaints flagged as possible harness artifacts (brawl smoke in `17`, cotton-ball blobs on water) were re-judged fresh against this gallery and did not reproduce. Two new set-pieces (whales, staged captain sinking) land cleanly and are genuine highlights. The reflection seam and rim-light fixes both read clearly on screen now. The karst tri-tone fix is real in the math (verified by re-running the hash in isolation) but still doesn't read in any of the 30 shots — every karst view here is a hazy establishing shot, and the fog is eating the tint before it reaches the sensor. One new, previously-unflagged issue: Age-5 ("Age of Swarms") chromatic aberration is tuned aggressively enough to fringe HUD nameplates at the frame edges.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 9/10.** The bar is genuinely AAA-adjacent in the wide shots — `01`, `13`, `15` could be marketing key art. The new whale pass (`26`, `27`) and captain-sinking sequence (`28`, `29`) are a real step up: modeled humpback anatomy (tubercled head, hooked dorsal, notched flukes), plausible blow/dive kinematics, and a three-act sinking (lurch → stern rears out of the sea → slide under) with magazine cook-offs and waterline venting that reads as directed, not generic. Two things hold it under a 10: karst per-stack tint (claim 4) is real in code but invisible on screen in all 30 shots — every karst view here is 150+ units out and fogged, so three stone types read as one; and the Age-5 chromatic-aberration grade (`23`, x:1230-1600,y:10-180) fringes rock silhouettes and even HUD nameplate text by 10-15px at the frame corners, which reads as a bug even though it's an intentional per-age grade escalation (`main.js:432`, `ca: 0.0013`).

**VFX Readability & Impact: 9/10.** Explosions (`11`, `21`), the drone swarm arc (`14`), the Leviathan's chomp-and-drag (`03`), and the captain-sinking cook-offs (`28`, `29`) all read instantly. Re-checked `17` and `16` for the Review-16 smoke complaint: `16`'s nameplates ("1 Yamamoto", "1 Barbarossa") are crisp white-on-dark at x:1370-1500,y:90-130, and the round objects on the water at x:120-200,y:280-320 in `16` are in-flight cannonballs and a snapped spar, not frozen spray blobs — the harness bug's symptoms are gone. Faction hue reads fine through combat smoke in every shot checked (e.g. the burning Crimson hull top-right of `20` is unmistakably red at a glance).

**UI/UX: 8/10.** Kill-feed, level-up banner, and nameplate fade all read cleanly, including in the two shots Review 16 flagged for smoke (`16`, `17` — both clear this pass). New ding: in `23` (Age of Swarms), the escalating chromatic-aberration grade smears "Ching Shih" and "Tromp"'s nameplate text and health-bar edges with a visible red/cyan double-image (x:1230-1600,y:250-320) — measured edge-channel separation there is ~30 vs. ~11-20 in every other shot sampled. HUD nameplates are drawn into the same render target as the post-process chain, so the late-game grade is now strong enough to hurt its own legibility.

**First-10-Seconds Wow: 9/10.** Unchanged and still excellent — `01`'s convoy-past-the-castle establishing shot, `06`'s menu with god-rays through the karst stacks, and `13`'s wider version of the same beat all look like a shipped game's title sequence, not a tech demo.

**Gameplay Camera Readability: 9/10.** Up from Review 16. The gunboat rim-light gain bump (`units.js:315`, `2.2`) is now unambiguous: cropping the unnamed escort hulls in `08` (x:480-660,y:510-610 and x:1160-1500,y:490-570) shows a clear blue Fresnel glow tracing the hull silhouette against the water, not the "confirmed present, barely visible" result from Review 16. Orbit/top-down views (`19`, `20`) stay crisp, and the reflection fix means distant water no longer breaks the read on karst silhouettes with a hard duplicate edge.

## Top 6 fixes, ranked by visual impact per hour of work

1. **Karst tri-tone is mathematically fixed but perceptually dead (`00`, `13`, `22`).** The hash now diverges correctly, but stoneKind 1/2 only desaturate toward grey (`environment.js:355-356`, lerp targets `0.72-1.1x` luminance, no real hue swap) and every shot with karst in it is fogged at range. Widen the tint spread (push `tintR`/`tintB` further apart in `warm`, and lerp stoneKind 1 toward an actual cool blue-grey hue rather than a grey-luminance match) so the effect has a chance of surviving `applyCloudShadow`'s atmospheric blend. Cheapest, highest-payoff item here — three review cycles have now confirmed the math is fine and the fog is what's killing it.
2. **Age-5 chromatic aberration fringes its own HUD text (`23`, x:1230-1600,y:250-320).** `main.js:432`'s `AGE_GRADE[5].ca: 0.0013` combined with the `(1.0 + edge * 6.0)` corner multiplier in `renderer.js:66` produces a measured ~30-unit R/G edge separation at frame corners vs. ~11-20 elsewhere — visible as red/cyan double-imaged nameplate text. Either cap the edge multiplier before Age 5's `ca` value, or drop `ca` at that tier from `0.0013` to something nearer `0.0009`; a one-line constant change.
3. **Reflection breakup could go one step further at the karst waterline (`15`, x:1045-1075,y:480-650).** The new ripple/smear (claim 1) has genuinely fixed the "razor-straight seam" complaint, but the boundary is still identifiable as a ~2px jagged line rather than fully dissolved into wave chop. Bumping `ocean.js:172`'s distance coefficient (currently `0.007`) slightly would finish the job on exactly the shot used to showcase the feature.
4. **No screenshot in this set gets close enough to a karst stack to prove out claim 4 either way.** Add a close-range karst test shot (camera within ~40 units of a stack cluster) to the gallery script; right now the tri-tone system can never be validated or invalidated by this pipeline, only inferred from source.
5. **Sinking-sequence hull silhouette gets visually noisy at the fire-burst frame (`28`, x:600-950,y:330-560).** The cook-off flash is large enough to wash out where the hull, sails, and waterline actually are for a couple of frames — still a strong beat, but tone the flash radius/intensity down 15-20% so the listing hull stays legible through it.
6. **Minor: whale blow mist could use a touch more sustain (`26`/`27`).** The initial burst column reads well, but the slow drifting mist afterward (`sealife.js`'s second `_blow` emit) is faint enough that a still frame after the first second or two loses the "just blew" read. A small alpha bump on the drifting puff (`a0: 0.16` in `_blow`) would keep the beat legible a moment longer without changing timing.

## Broken

- The Age-5 chromatic-aberration overtune (`23`, top fix #2) is a genuine rendering issue, not a capture artifact — it's a deliberate per-age constant that happens to be strong enough to blur its own UI at the resolution/aspect ratio tested.
- No other genuine rendering bugs found this pass. The Review 15/16 reflection seam and the Review 16 karst-hash bug are both resolved at the code level (seam: fully; karst hash: fully, though its downstream visual effect is currently too weak to matter — see top fix #1, which is a tuning gap, not a bug).
- Confirmed as harness artifacts, not game bugs: Review 16's "dense brawl smoke crushing sail hue" and "cotton-ball blobs on the water" in the `17`-equivalent shot did not reproduce in this gallery; the round objects previously suspected as frozen spray are in-flight cannonballs and debris.
