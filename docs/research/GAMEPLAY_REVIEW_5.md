**Feel — 9/10 (unchanged).** Both of R4's blockers for a 10 are now fixed and verified: the hypersonic strike ring is hostile-aware (`abilities.js:66-68`, `hostileH ? 0xff8a00 : G.teamGlow(h.team)`, matching the barrage branch's existing check at line 74), and the Help copy's "Red rings" line now reads "Orange rings" (`index.html:34`, also `:58`). On top of that, this round adds real production value: captains now sink in three overlapping acts — lurch/list (0-1.1s), stern rearing (1.0-4.4s cubic/smoothstep blend), then the slide under (4.0-6.9s, quadratic ease) — verified in `units.js:121-129`, hero-only (gunboats/structures still use the older single-stage sink at `units.js:130-134`). Your own death now sweeps a low-pass to 380 Hz over 1.3s while music stays on its own bus (`audio/audio.js:90-97,264-269`, `game.js:608`), and a free camera orbit (`core/camera.js:29-34`, `Z`/`X`/middle-drag/shift-drag, `C` to reset) adds a small but real degree of player agency at zero cost to readability. Held at 9, not 10, because none of this was verified visually this round (code-read only, no screenshot pass), and the sink timings for gunboats/structures still look comparatively flat next to the new hero sequence.

**Strategic Depth — 9/10 (+1).** Hull counters stopped being cosmetic and became a real combat rule this round: `combat.js:90-97` now multiplies damage by `COUNTER_BONUS` (1.2, `config.js:51`) whenever a hero's hull counters its target's, with a gold "COUNTER +20%" float text gated to once per 8s (`combat.js:95`) and, new, a warning hint shown to the countered player once per 30s (`combat.js:96`, `"<hull> counters your hull · it deals +20% damage to you"`). Before this commit (`ba80c55`, confirmed via `git log -S`) the same `COUNTERS` graph only drove the age-choice card's "Strong vs / Weak to" text and the bots' pick score — it had no effect on damage. This closes a real gap: counter-picking now has teeth, and a countered player gets a legible, actionable warning instead of just losing a fight they can't explain. Held back from 10: `hud.js:190-192`'s `liveMatch` is still a raw 1:1 enemy-hull tally with no HP/level weighting (R3 improvement #4, three rounds open now), and the Dusk Tide remains a forced timer, not a player decision.

**Ability/Hull Design — 8/10 (unchanged).** This round's third tuning pass on Arsenal vs Battleship (`config.js:174` Radar Salvo count 6→7 buffing Battleship; `config.js:197` Railgun cd 10→12 nerfing Arsenal; `config.js:209` Micro Swarm dmg 48→43 nerfing Mothership — all three confirmed via `git show ba8c963`) **moved the sim numbers the wrong way**: Arsenal (n=9) now leads Battleship (n=32) **9.22 vs 2.84 kills/instance (3.25x, was 2.66x in R4)**, and the damage ratio also widened, **69,357 vs 39,931 (1.74x, was 1.68x)**. This is the third consecutive round this exact matchup has been flagged, and the third round the fix has undershot or moved backward. Samples are still thin (Battleship n=32 is solid; Arsenal n=9 is not) so this could be partly noise, but the consistent direction across three independent tuning attempts — nerf Arsenal, buff Battleship, still nothing moves — suggests the diagnosis (Railgun cooldown, Radar Salvo count) is not where the actual power gap lives. Mothership did move the right way this round (12.0 kills/inst, down from 13.5) after the Micro Swarm nerf, and its pick rate improved (9:5 Arsenal:Mothership this round vs. R4's much more extreme avoidance) — one real win inside an otherwise flat category.

**Pacing & Balance — 9/10 (+1).** The new one-lane citadel exposure under the Dusk Tide (`game.js:160`, `mine.filter(...).length < (this.duskTide ? 1 : 2)`, landed via commit `8e2d98d`) plus the citadel exposure damage ramp (`combat.js:63`, 25% rising to 100% over 8s so an exposure is a moment, not an instant loss) plus the fort-damage multiplier now scaling continuously with time instead of a hard switch (`combat.js:70`, `1.7 + max(0, t-480)/90*0.5`, capped 2.4) are all doing real, replicated work: timeout endings fell again, from R4's 5/8 (62.5%) to **3/8 (37.5%)** this round, and all 5 citadel finishes (486, 487, 488, 491, 571s) landed inside the Dusk Tide window, most within the first 11 seconds of it — the exposed-lane rule is now the dominant way stalled games resolve. Held back from 10: of the 3 remaining timeouts, one still ends in a near-exact tie (`finalScore` [51.23, 51.03], a 0.19-point margin) — R4 improvement #9 ("push scale with time") clearly cut the *frequency* of stalemates but hasn't eliminated the closest ones. Also worth a note for the design doc: the ramp formula only reaches +136.7% by the 600s match end (never the GDD's stated "+140% by 9:30" — at 570s it's actually +120%; +140% would need t=606s, past the match's hard end), a small but real doc/code mismatch.

**Bot AI — 9/10 (+1).** The update-order fairness bug R4 flagged as "a real regression risk" is fixed, and fixed twice — first as a frame-parity alternation (`ba8c963`), extended to heroes/creeps/structures (`117ec11`), then upgraded to a fresh per-tick coin flip (`game.js:509`, `const rev = Math.random() < 0.5`) after a further commit (`ba80c55`) found that frame parity still handed one team a persistent first-mover edge (whole-tick gun cooldowns line up with frame parity). All four unit collections — bots (`:510`), heroes (`:513`), creeps (`:524`) and structures (`:525`) — now share the same per-tick coin, closing the exact mechanism R4's analysis identified. This round's sample shows team-0 winning 5/8 raw team wins (not team1 5/8 as in R4) — a reversal of direction rather than a persistting bias, and well within noise for n=8 (a 5-3 split has ~70% probability under a fair coin), consistent with the fix actually removing the systematic tilt rather than just changing its sign. Telegraph-dodge behavior is stable and reads as intended: hero hits inside a live hostile `G.dangers` zone happened 47.3% of the time (3,599/7,610) this round, essentially unchanged from R4's 50.2% — bots dodge some, not all, as designed. Held back from 10: `bot.js:299` (in `src/game/ai/bot.js`) still scores counter-picks by raw on-screen matchup count only, unweighted by which hull is actually over-performing (R3 improvement #4, still open, and it's the same mechanism behind this round's Arsenal-over-Battleship persistence — bots have no way to learn from live sim outcomes, only from the fixed graph).

**Onboarding — 10/10 (+1).** Both R4 blockers are resolved (stale "Red rings" line, above) and this round adds a proper first-run flow on top of the fix: a four-step "HOW TO PLAY" card (`index.html:45-78`) with icon illustrations for Sail/Fight/Grow/Win, a camera-controls line, and a persistent `?` button (`index.html:79`, tooltip "How to play (H)"). It auto-shows once per browser via a `localStorage` flag (`main.js:237`, `howtoPending = !automated && localGet('aa.howto') !== '1'`) after a short delay once the cinematic intro clears (`main.js:462`, gated on `!cameraDir.cine`), is reachable any time via `H` or `F1` (`main.js:383`), and any keypress dismisses it except modifier keys that would otherwise feel like an accidental miss-click (`main.js:377-380`, Tab/Alt/Shift/Control/Meta excluded). This is a well-built, low-friction tutorial layer with no remaining known gap found in this round's code read.

## Top 10 remaining improvements toward "exceptional"

1. **Re-diagnose the Arsenal vs Battleship gap instead of re-tuning the same two numbers a fourth time.** Three straight rounds of nerfing Railgun cooldown / buffing Radar Salvo count have moved the ratio the wrong way each time (2.83x→2.66x→3.25x kills/inst). Before touching `config.js:174,197` again, log per-ability damage share in a sim run (which ability is actually carrying Arsenal's kills — likely `hypersonic` or `salvo`, not `railgun`) so the next cut lands on the real lever.
2. **Weight the bot counter-pick score by live performance, not just the static COUNTERS graph.** `bot.js:299` (`src/game/ai/bot.js`) still only counts on-screen matchups; feed it a rolling kills/inst or dmg/inst signal per hull (even a hardcoded prior from these sim rounds) so bots stop under-picking Mothership relative to its output (9:5 Arsenal:Mothership this round despite Mothership's higher kills/inst).
3. **Weight `liveMatch`'s enemy-hull count by threat, not a flat 1:1 tally.** `hud.js:190-192` — three review rounds open (R3 improvement #4). Weight by `(level+1)` or hp fraction so the age-choice card's "Enemy fleet now" line reflects real danger, not headcount.
4. **Close the near-tie tail on the Dusk Tide timeout.** One of this round's 3 timeouts still ended `finalScore` [51.23, 51.03] — a 0.19-point margin. The continuous ramp (`combat.js:70`) helped the average case a lot (timeouts 62.5%→37.5%) but the closest games still coast; consider adding a small citadel-damage bonus for the trailing team specifically in the last 60s, or steepening the ramp's tail (raise the cap past 2.4, or shorten the 90s ramp window).
5. **Fix the GDD/code mismatch on the Dusk Tide ramp.** `GDD.md:34` says "+140% by 9:30"; the formula (`combat.js:70`) actually delivers +120% at 9:30 and only reaches +136.7% by the match's hard 600s end (`config.js:10`), never +140% in a real match. Either retune the formula's rate/cap or fix the GDD number — small, but the next tuning pass will read the doc and be misled by it.
6. **Add per-age balance data.** Age III hulls (Dreadnought/Torpedo) still have essentially no final-hull sample after 5 review rounds of full-length sims (0-3 instances each) because heroes age past them before the match ends. R4 improvement #7 (a `MATCH.duration`-capped sim mode, e.g. ~250s) is still unimplemented and is the only way to validate Age III parity.
7. **Give the countered-player warning hint (`combat.js:96`) a matching positive cue.** The new system tells you when you're being countered but a captain who's *counter-picked well* only sees the same gold "+20%" float the attacker sees — a short "you counter this hull" indicator when you first engage a hull you counter would close the loop and reinforce the new mechanic's teaching value.
8. **Bring gunboat/structure sinking up to the new hero standard.** `units.js:130-134`'s single-stage sink (flat quadratic drop, no lurch/rear stages) now reads noticeably flatter next to the new three-act hero sequence (`:121-129`) in the same fight — even a two-stage version (lurch + slide, skip the stern-rear) would close the gap cheaply for gunboats, which die constantly and are the most-seen sinking animation in a match.
9. **Validate the update-order fix with a larger, evenly-split batch.** This round's n=8 (team 0 winning 5/8) doesn't confirm the systemic bias is gone, only that it isn't obviously still there in the same direction. R4 improvement #10 (a ~50-match batch, 25/25 split across `&team=0`/`&team=1`) is still the only way to close this out with confidence — three rounds now (R3, R4, R5) have each found a different-looking but statistically inconclusive skew.
10. **Verify this round's Feel and Onboarding claims visually, not just in code.** Both the hypersonic-ring fix and the staged-sinking sequence read correctly in the source, but neither was confirmed with a screenshot/video pass this round (the harness available, `sim4.mjs`, only reports gameplay state, not pixels). A short photo-mode pass — e.g. force a hero kill under `?autopilot=1` and capture frames through the 6.9s sink — would catch any visual regression code-reading can't (z-fighting, the new orbit camera clipping through geography, etc.).
