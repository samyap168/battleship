# Armada Ascension — Art Review 10

Reviewed all 17 images in `tests/output/gallery10/` against `gallery9` and the Round 9 write-up, with PIL matched-frame diffs (`02_reforging` at the same 9:44 state, `10_island`/`13_opening_b` cross-round crops) plus direct color sampling of the reforging pillar and the `evidence/reforge_pillar_before_after.png` capture. ## Scores

**Visual Quality: 9.6/10 (+0.2).** The ascension pillar recolor is real and it's the single biggest visual win this round. Color-sampling `02_reforging` at matched off-axis pixels (x=795±100, y=180, away from the saturated core) shows Round 9 reading cool cyan (sampled pixel `(102,114,120)`, B>R) versus Round 10's `(173,139,90)` at the identical pixel, R exceeding B by 83 — an unambiguous warm-gold shift, and the `evidence/reforge_pillar_before_after.png` matched 9:44 capture shows it side by side: the pillar is now visibly amber against the still-blue hex rings, exactly separating the two effects Round 9 (mis-)flagged as merged. Ship flags are also confirmably 1.35x larger in `flagGeo` (`len *= 1.35; h *= 1.35`), and `04_battle_closeup` and `17_zoomed_in_gameplay` both show pennants and mast flags large enough to read cloth silhouette at gameplay-camera distance. Held back from higher: the pillar's claimed narrowing (width 8→5.5) doesn't survive the bloom pass — core pixels at y=160-180 blow out to the same near-white in both rounds (core width 27-33px either way), so only the color half of that fix is pixel-verifiable; and two Round 9 carryovers are untouched — the foliage-cluster fix is still confirmed on one tree only (`10_island` diffs at 1.67% vs Round 9, i.e. unchanged), and the near-citadel karst ring in `01`/`13` (x:0-260) is still uniform fat columns, re-confirmed by fresh crop this round.

**VFX Readability: 9.7/10 (+0.2).** Hit markers are a clean, well-executed addition — a tight crop of `04_battle_closeup` (x:780-950,y:560-680) shows crisp white X-ticks with black outline sitting directly on the target ship, exactly matching `hud.js`'s corner-tick geometry. Danger rings are real too: `09_storm` shows a tan/gold ring around the player's own ship and a distinct red ring around the enemy `Ching Shih`, confirming the `hostile ? 0xff3a2a : teamGlow` branch in `abilities.js` fires correctly. One new readability gap surfaces from this same shot: the red danger ring sits on a Crimson-team (red-hulled) enemy, and at a glance the ring's red is close enough to that team's own hull/health-bar red that it takes a second look to separate "incoming barrage" from "that's just an enemy ship" — see Fix #2 below. Flags read as cloth (not painted plank) at the sizes checked, continuing Round 9's win.

**UI/UX: 9.8/10 (+0.2).** The Admiral's Orders panel is the standout addition this round — a single top-left panel (`#objectives`, 270px, no overlap with any other HUD element) that correctly shows just an age-progress row early (`02`, `07`), all three rows — squall countdown, Leviathan countdown, age progress — together at `08`, and the "Press T" hot state once affordable (`03`, `05`). It's legible, doesn't collide with hints (bottom-center) or the minimap, and every row sampled matches its live game-state numbers exactly (e.g. `08`'s "Age of Airpower 1352/2200" against the gold counter shown lower on the same frame). The age-choice cards' matchup lines are now genuinely generated from the shared `COUNTERS` graph rather than hand-written strings that could drift from real hull names — a side-by-side crop of `05_age_choice` against Round 9 shows Round 9's cards said "Strong vs Torpedo Cruisers and Carriers" while Round 10 correctly says "Strong vs Fleet Carrier and Mothership" for the same slot, i.e. it's reading real data now, not prose. The live enemy-fleet count line exists in code (`liveMatch`) but didn't fire in the one `05` capture available (no enemy fleet member matched either card's counter list at that game moment) — not a bug, just unverified in this gallery.

**First-10-Seconds Wow Factor: 9.7/10 (+0.0).** None of this round's changes touch the hero shots. `01_opening_cinematic`'s beam/karst/spire pixels are unchanged to within noise (beam region diff mean 0.11, essentially bit-identical), and `06_menu`/`13_opening_b` show no new content. Nothing regressed, but nothing new landed here either — this round's work (pillar, hit markers, danger rings, HUD panel) is entirely mid-match and gameplay-facing, so the shots a trailer would use are exactly where Round 9 left them.

## What's shippable vs. what's still holding it back

**Shippable now:** the ascension pillar's gold recolor (`02`, confirmed by direct pixel sampling and the before/after evidence); hit markers (`04`, crisp and correctly colored); danger rings distinguishing hostile from ally intent (`09`); the Admiral's Orders panel across every game state sampled (`02,03,05,07,08`); data-driven matchup lines on age cards (`05`).

**Still holding it back:** the citadel's own structural light column (`applySoftBeam`) is unchanged since Round 8 and, now that the pillar confusion is resolved, is next in line as the visible weak point in `01`/`13`/`15`; the red danger ring's hue collision with Crimson-team red; foliage-cluster and near-citadel-karst carryovers from Round 9 remain exactly as they were, unaddressed this round.

## Top 8 Remaining Fixes (ranked by impact)

1. **Citadel light column still has no real color identity of its own.** `01`, `13`, `15` all show it as a blown-out near-white spike (core RGB ~251-255 across all channels) that reads as generic bloom, not a team-colored beacon. Recipe: in `applySoftBeam` (`materials.js`), cap `diffuseColor.rgb` luminance to ~0.85 before the tint multiply so the HDR bloom pass can't wash the color back to white — `diffuseColor.rgb = min(diffuseColor.rgb, vec3(0.85)) * (1.0 + (1.0 - smoothstep(40.5, 50.0, vBmY)) * teamTint)` — then re-run the same off-core pixel sampling used on `02`'s pillar this round to confirm the hue survives.
2. **Danger ring red collides with Crimson team red.** `09_storm`'s ring around `Ching Shih` is legible but close in hue to that team's own ship/health-bar red — a split-second read cost in the exact moment (incoming barrage) where speed matters. Recipe: in `abilities.js`, shift the hostile ring to a color outside the team palette, e.g. `0xff8a00` (danger-orange) or add a fast white/black dash pattern to the ring shader so it silhouettes against any hull color underneath it.
3. **Foliage cluster consistency is still unresolved.** `10_island`'s isolated hero tree is fixed (per Round 9), but this round's matched diff against Round 9 is 1.67% — essentially untouched — meaning the denser cluster 2-3 trees over still reads as round-blob crowns. Recipe: audit whether cloud-pruned pad generation is gated behind a seed branch that only some tree instances take; make it the default path.
4. **Near-citadel karst ring is still uniform fat columns.** Fresh crops of `01` and `13` at x:0-260 this round show the same stout, undifferentiated towers Round 9 flagged; the radius-scale variety fix clearly stops before this ring. Recipe: extend the `0.55-1.0` random radius-scale to the near-tower spawn loop's first ring, not just the band one loop out.
5. **The pillar's narrowing (8→5.5) isn't verifiable in pixels.** The color shift is confirmed, but sampling `02`'s beam core at y=160-180 shows near-identical core width (27-33px) in both rounds because bloom, not underlying geometry, dominates the visible column width. Recipe: reduce the bloom threshold or base alpha specifically on the `pillar` beam instance so the narrower true width actually reads at the pixel level, then re-diff.
6. **Live enemy-fleet count line on age cards is unverified.** The `liveMatch` code path in `hud.js` looks correct, but `05_age_choice`'s one capture shows no enemy fleet member matching either card's counter list, so the line never renders in the gallery. Recipe: capture one `05` shot at a game moment with a live enemy of a countered/countering hull present, and confirm the "Enemy fleet now: N it counters · N that counter it" line actually draws.
7. **Debris embers remain modest at native resolution.** No change this round (`spawnDebris` untouched); Round 9's fix is real in a tight crop of `11_explosion` but still subtle at gallery viewing size. Recipe: as Round 9 suggested, push `s0`/`s1` base scale another ~20%.
8. **Flag-size claim needs a deterministic capture to confirm pixel-for-pixel.** The `1.35x` scale-up in `flagGeo` is unambiguous in source and flags read as cloth in `04`/`17`, but combat framing differs run to run, so a true matched-frame width measurement (the discipline that closed HUD sheen) hasn't been done yet. Recipe: add one paused, fixed-camera capture of a single ship's masthead flag for a clean before/after width diff.

## The one change to reach 9.7+ Visual

**Give the citadel's own light column an HDR-safe color identity.** It's the last piece of the beam confusion Round 8/9 were chasing, now that the ageUp pillar (the actual culprit) is fixed — and it's the one still standing in three of the game's most-seen establishing shots (`01`, `13`, `15`). The fix is mechanically the same discipline that made this round's pillar recolor verifiable: cap luminance before the tint multiply so bloom can't erase the hue, then confirm with the same off-core pixel sampling used here.

## Verdict

Round 10 is a genuine, well-verified round across three of four categories. The ascension pillar recolor closes out a two-round-old, previously-misdiagnosed complaint with clean supporting pixels; hit markers and danger rings are exactly the combat-clarity features a MOBA needs and both hold up under crop inspection; the Admiral's Orders panel is the most useful single HUD addition since sheen was closed out, and it's correct across every game state sampled. Nothing this round touches the first-10-seconds hero shots, so Wow holds flat rather than regressing or improving. The one new problem worth fixing — red danger rings blending into Crimson's own red — is a small, cheap fix. The bigger remaining piece is the citadel's own beam, now clearly isolated as the true next target rather than confused with the pillar that got fixed this round.
