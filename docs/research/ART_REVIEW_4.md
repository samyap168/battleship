# Armada Ascension — Art Review 4

Reviewed: `tests/output/gallery4/01_opening_cinematic.png` → `12_reflections.png` against Round 3 (`gallery3`) and Round 3's written review. SwiftShader noise, harness title cards, and stale hints ignored per brief. Claimed changes verified pixel-by-pixel where possible (crop + brightness/clip sampling), not taken on faith.

## Scores

**Visual Quality: 7.5/10 (-0.5).** Real, measurable gains: the Leviathan (03) now has genuine scale/plate detailing instead of a smooth tube, the dreadnought/ironclad hulls (07, 08) show actual rust-streak and waterline-grime texture under zoom, and fleet-zoom ship silhouettes (07, 08) are now individually readable — a 3-round-old defect finally closed. But `01_opening_cinematic.png`, the single most important frame in the build, is catastrophically broken: pixel sampling confirms columns 0–1100 (≈72% of the 1600px frame) are pure `RGB(0,0,0)` with real content squeezed into a narrow right-hand strip. This is not the old HDR clip bug (that one is gone — see VFX) — it's a new viewport/canvas-sizing bug, and it is worse than what it replaced. `12_reflections.png`, ostensibly the same establishing shot, renders correctly and looks great, which means the bug is non-deterministic — an unpredictable regression, not a consistent one, which is the harder failure mode to ship around.

**VFX Readability: 8.5/10 (+1.0).** The Round-1-era tonemap clipping is measurably fixed: sampled pure-white-clipped pixels in the explosion dropped from 0.75% (gallery3/11) to 0.0% (gallery4/11), and the fireball now reads as a shaped orange/tan core with soot, not a blown white disc. Storm rain (09) is confirmed as varied per-instance streaks (length/angle/opacity all differ) rather than uniform repeating lines. Cellular-foam wakes (07, 08) are visibly mottled/turbulent versus Round 3's smoother triangular wedge. Ability-icon cooldowns (04, 07, 08) now carry a real radial-progress mask instead of bare numerals — a 2-round-old fix finally shipped, even if the execution is rough (see fixes).

**UI/UX: 9/10 (+0.5).** The cooldown wipe is a genuine functional upgrade over raw numbers. Kill-feed and objective messaging (07, 08) remain clean and well-hierarchized. Held back by the Armory/ability HUD panels (07, 08), which under zoom are still confirmed flat alpha-filled rectangles with a gold hairline border and zero baked material — the exact defect flagged in Round 2 fix #1 and Round 3 fix #7, now unaddressed two rounds running.

**First-10-Seconds Wow Factor: 5.5/10 (-1.5).** This category lives or dies on the opening cinematic, and this round's actual `01` capture is a near-black frame for most of its width — an objectively worse first impression than Round 3's blown-out-but-visible sky. `12_reflections` proves the *intended* shot (letterboxed sunrise, castle, fleet, soft sky, water tint) is strong and would score 8+, but since the same establishing shot renders two different ways in the same build, the wow score has to reflect the version that's actually shipping some fraction of the time.

## What's now shippable vs. what's still holding it back

**Shippable now:** Leviathan boss detail and encounter framing (03); fleet-zoom ship silhouette readability (07, 08) — closes a 3-round-open issue; the explosion tonemap/bloom fix (11), verified by direct pixel sampling; cellular-foam wakes (04, 07, 08); varied storm rain streaks (09); the radial cooldown-wipe concept (04, 07, 08); age-choice cards and menu archipelago composition (05, 06), carried over from Round 3; the reflections establishing shot *when it renders correctly* (12).

**Still holding it back:** the non-deterministic black-frame bug in the actual `01_opening_cinematic` capture (P0 — see fix #1); foliage (10) still reads as smooth cauliflower-blob canopies with no visible leaf-cluster breakup or backlit translucency in this lighting condition; HUD panel material still a flat fill after two review rounds; planar reflections that, on close inspection, contribute sky-tint and horizon glow but no legible mirrored silhouette of geometry; hull weathering that is real but too low-contrast to register at normal gameplay zoom, only visible on a director's crop.

## Top 8 Remaining Fixes (ranked by impact)

1. **Fix the non-deterministic black-frame bug in the opening cinematic (01).** Confirmed via pixel sampling: ~72% of the frame is solid `(0,0,0)` with content confined to a narrow right-side rectangle, while `12_reflections` (same scene) renders full-frame correctly. This points to a canvas/composer resize race: the renderer or `EffectComposer` is sized before the container reaches its final layout width, or `gl.viewport`/`gl.scissor` is left set to a stale rect from a previous frame size. Recipe: drive resize off a `ResizeObserver` on the actual canvas parent (not just `window.resize`), call `renderer.setSize(w, h, false)` and `composer.setSize(w, h)` together in the same call, update `camera.aspect` + `camera.updateProjectionMatrix()` before the next render, and don't allow a screenshot/capture to fire until 2 RAF frames after any resize event settles.
2. **Clean up the cooldown radial-wipe shader (04, 07, 08).** The mask is implemented but reads as a hard vertical split rather than a clockwise radial sweep, and the numeral (e.g. "02" on the Q icon in 04) visually collides with the icon glyph underneath it. Recipe: `float a = atan(uv.x-0.5, 0.5-uv.y); a = a<0.0 ? a+TWO_PI : a; if (a < progress*TWO_PI) color.rgb *= 0.25;` pivoting from 12 o'clock, add a 2px bright rim exactly at the wipe edge, and shrink the numeral to a small corner badge instead of centering it over the icon.
3. **Bake real material into HUD panel bodies (07, 08 Armory/ability bar) — unaddressed 2 rounds running.** Confirmed flat fill under 3x zoom: no rivets, no wood grain, no brass hairline beyond the single 1px border. Recipe unchanged from Round 3: `CanvasTexture`-bake a riveted-brass/dark-walnut normal+AO map once at load, sample it behind the existing gold corner bracket with a soft specular term.
4. **Close-range foliage still lacks the claimed leaf-clump/backlit translucency (10).** At this camera angle canopies are still single smooth "cauliflower" blobs — no per-cluster silhouette variance, no rim-glow visible. Recipe: build each canopy from 6–10 randomly offset/rotated/scaled small blob clusters instead of one sphere, and add `vec3 sss = lightColor * pow(max(dot(-lightDir, viewDir), 0.0), 2.0) * translucency;` to the diffuse term so edges glow green when backlit — then re-capture with the sun placed behind the tree line to actually verify it, since this frame's lighting angle can't confirm or deny the shader exists.
5. **Make planar reflections legible, not just tinting (12).** Zoomed crops directly beneath the castle and lighthouse show no recognizable mirrored silhouette of the towers/blue roofs — only a sky-colored wave texture. Recipe: verify the reflection pass is actually rendering mirrored scene geometry to an offscreen target (not just sampling an env-map), composite with `mix(baseColor, reflectionSample, pow(1.0-dot(normal,viewDir), 5.0))`, and reduce wave-normal distortion strength so structure silhouettes survive near grazing angles instead of dissolving into noise.
6. **Increase hull-weathering contrast for normal gameplay distance (07, 08).** Rust streaks and waterline grime are real (confirmed under 5x crop on the dreadnought) but nearly invisible at the default battle-cam zoom used in 07/08 — the hull still reads as a flat brown fill in the un-cropped shot. Recipe: raise the rust-mask opacity ~40% and darken the waterline grime band specifically; it should be legible as a distinct value read without director-zoom.
7. **Extend faction color beyond the hull pinstripe (07, 08).** Ships are now individually readable, but the only friend/foe cue is a thin blue/red stripe along the hull — in a smoke-filled brawl (04) that's a slow read. Recipe: add a fresnel-driven rim-light tint (`pow(1.0-dot(N,V),3.0) * factionColor`) over the whole hull so faction reads via silhouette glow, not just a 2px line.
8. **Add the missing sea-impact rings to the storm rain (09).** Streaks themselves are confirmed improved and varied, but no ring/ripple decals appear where drops meet the water, despite that being part of the stated change. Recipe: spawn short-lived (~0.3s) expanding additive ring sprites (radius 0→0.4m, opacity 1→0, ease-out) at a small Poisson-sampled set of near-camera surface points, driven by the same density uniform already feeding the rain instancer.

## Verdict

This round shows a team that fixed the things it said it fixed — the explosion/bloom clipping is gone by direct pixel measurement, the Leviathan has real skin detail, wakes are turbulent instead of static, and the 3-round-old fleet-zoom ship-blob problem is finally closed. That's substantive, verifiable progress, not just claims. But it shipped alongside a new failure mode that is arguably worse than what it replaced: the actual opening-cinematic capture is two-thirds solid black, and — more alarming for a team about to call this done — the identical shot renders perfectly in a sibling capture from the same build. A funded AAA studio's QA would flag a non-deterministic black-screen-on-launch bug as a stop-ship P0 before anyone even looked at foliage shaders or HUD brass textures. Fix #1 first, then this build's genuine VFX and boss-encounter gains will actually be visible to the player who's supposed to see them.
