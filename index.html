Earn gold from gunboats, captains, forts and trade ports. Each age reforges your hull: Sail → Steam → Dreadnought → Airpower → Swarm. Ages III-V offer a choice of two warships. Break two lanes (outer then inner fort) to expose the enemy citadel. At dusk (10:00) the team with the most forts destroyed, kills and citadel damage wins. Counters deal +20% damage: Torpedo beats Dreadnought, Battleship and Arsenal; Carrier beats Dreadnought and Torpedo; Battleship beats Carrier and Mothership; Arsenal beats Carrier and Battleship; Mothership beats Arsenal, Dreadnought and Torpedo.
HOW TO PLAY
Four things, thirty seconds
1 · SAIL
Click the sea to sail there. Click an enemy to attack. Your guns fire on their own.
2 · FIGHT
Hold QWER to aim, release to fire. Sail out of orange rings: incoming shells.
3 · GROW
Sink gunboats and captains for gold. Press T to advance an age: Sail → Swarm.
4 · WIN
Push with your gunboats. Break two forts in a lane to expose the enemy citadel, then sink it.
